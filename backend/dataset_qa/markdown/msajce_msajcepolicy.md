A scale of 5 (high) through 1 (low) is used to rate the quality of teaching in online feedback.

## Section: 8. Termination of Service / Resignation & 9. Leave Rules & 10. Casual Leave (CL)

### Question 1 (Factoid)
What are the reasons for termination of service at MSAJCE?

**Answer**:
The Chairman / Appointing Authority has the power to terminate the services of a member of MSAJCE for any of the following reasons: Serious misconduct and willful negligence of duty, Gross insubordination, Physical or mental unfitness, Participation in any criminal offence involving moral turpitude, or if the efficiency of an employee has been impaired due to any infirmity.

---
### Question 2 (Factoid)
What is the notice period for termination of service for a staff member on temporary appointments or during probationary period?

**Answer**:
One month notice or one month salary in lieu thereof.

---
### Question 3 (Factoid)
What is the notice period for termination of service for a staff member on permanent service?

**Answer**:
One month notice or one month salary in lieu thereof.

---
### Question 4 (Factoid)
What is the Casual Leave (CL) policy at MSAJCE?

**Answer**:
All employees are eligible for 12 days of Casual leave per year at the rate of 1 day per working month during the year from 1st July to 30th June.

---
### Question 5 (Factoid)
How many Casual Leave (CL) days can be granted at a time?

**Answer**:
Not more than 3 CL shall be granted.

---
### Question 6 (Factoid)
Can Casual Leave (CL) be carried over to the next academic year?

**Answer**:
No, carryover of lapsed CL to next academic year is not permissible.

---
### Question 7 (Procedural)
What is the procedure for short absence not exceeding one hour on any working day?

**Answer**:
Permission for short absence not exceeding one hour on any working day either at the beginning of working hour, end of working hour or in the middle may be granted at the discretion of the Principal / Designated Authority for two occurrences per month.

---
### Question 8 (Factoid)
What happens if the number of permissions for short absence exceeds more than two in a month?

**Answer**:
It shall be considered as half day (1/2 day) CL for each permission exceeding two.

---
### Question 9 (Factoid)

**Answer**:
Excess CL taken will be considered LOP at the time of settlement.

**Answer**:
No, CL cannot be combined with any other leaves except SL and Compensation Off.

**Answer**:
If the patent is taken jointly with a sponsoring agency, the patenting cost will be equally shared.

30% of the total revenue accruing from the commercial exploitation of IP owned by MSAJCE shall be credited to inventors.

**Answer**:
70% of the revenue shall be credited to MSAJCE.

MSAJCE bears the charges incurred for processing and acquisition of intellectual property rights.

---
### Question 7 (Factoid)

**Answer**:
MSAJCE bears the maintenance charges for the first 7 years or till commercialization, whichever is earlier.

---
### Question 8 (Yes_no)

**Answer**:
Yes, MSAJCE shall anytime share the ownership of the IP with the prior consent with the inventor.

### Question 9 (Factoid)
