## Section: Kaushal Augmentation and Restructuring Mission of AICTE (KARMA) & 1. Overview and Objectives & 2. Skilling Models & 3. College Implementation and Approvals

### Question 1 (Factoid)
What is the name of the AICTE initiative for skill development?

**Answer**:
The Kaushal Augmentation and Restructuring Mission of AICTE (KARMA)

---
### Question 2 (Exploratory)
What is the primary objective of the KARMA initiative?

**Answer**:
To overcome the dual challenge of the scarcity of skilled manpower in jobs and the low skill level of those who are presently employed.

---
### Question 3 (Factoid)
What is the focus of the training provided under the KARMA scheme?

**Answer**:
Domain-specific, demand-led skill training activities, preferably in core engineering sectors, leading directly to employment.

---
### Question 4 (Factoid)
What is the eligibility criteria for candidates to participate in the KARMA scheme?

**Answer**:
Candidates who have passed at least the tenth standard or completed ITI courses.

---
### Question 5 (Exploratory)
What are the three models proposed by AICTE for the skilling of in-school students, school dropouts, and students pursuing technical education?

**Answer**:
Model 1 targets school dropouts, Model 2 targets advanced-level skilling for students already studying in AICTE-approved technical institutions, and Model 3 integrates school education with higher education to skill students.

---
### Question 6 (Factoid)
What are the two courses approved by AICTE for Mohamed Sathak A.J. College of Engineering under Model 1?

**Answer**:
Computer Hardware Network Maintenance and Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technician.
