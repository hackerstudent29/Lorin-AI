### Question 1 (Factoid)
When was the Mohamed Sathak Trust established?

**Answer**:
The Mohamed Sathak Trust was established on the 26th of October 1973.

---
### Question 2 (Factoid)
What was the primary purpose of establishing the Mohamed Sathak Trust?

**Answer**:
The primary purpose of establishing the Mohamed Sathak Trust was to help deprived people from rural and financially challenged backgrounds to obtain quality education.

---
### Question 3 (Factoid)
What was the first self-financing engineering college established by the Mohamed Sathak Trust?

**Answer**:
The first self-financing engineering college in the country was established by the Trust in 1984 at Kilakarai.

---
### Question 4 (Factoid)
How many educational institutions does the Mohamed Sathak Trust presently have?

**Answer**:
The trust presently has eighteen educational institutions across Kilakarai, Ramanathapuram, and Chennai.

---
### Question 5 (Factoid)
Who are the founders of MSAJCE?

**Answer**:
The founders of MSAJCE include Late Alhaj Dr. S. M. Dasthagir, Late Hajjani Syed Hameeda Beevi, Late Alhaj Dr. P.R.L. Shaik Aboobacker, Late Alhaj S.M. Ahamed Jalaluddin, Late Alhaj S. M. Kabeer, and Late Alhaj Dr. S. M. Hamid Abdul Quadir.

---
### Question 6 (Factoid)
Who serves as the Chairman of the trustees guiding MSAJCE?

**Answer**:
Alhaj Janab S. M. Yousuf serves as the Chairman.

---
### Question 7 (Factoid)
What is the guiding philosophy of the Mohamed Sathak Trust?

**Answer**:
The guiding philosophy of the trust is that God will not change the condition of a people until they try to change themselves.

---
### Question 8 (Exploratory)
What is the objective of the Mohamed Sathak Trust?

**Answer**:
The trust focuses on doing the right things without any bias and, with a charitable mind, continuously strives to build an institution of academic excellence that helps in building the nation.
