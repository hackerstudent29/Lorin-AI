What are the key industry drivers for the growth of the semiconductor industry in India?

**Answer**:
The growth of the semiconductor industry in India is driven by the National Policy on Electronics, large-scale investments by companies like Intel, TSMC, Samsung, and Global Foundries, and the roll-out of 5G networks and planning for 6G.

---
### Question 2 (Factoid)
What skills are in high demand for VLSI design and fabrication?

**Answer**:
Proficiency in design tools such as Cadence, Mentor Graphics, Synopsys, and Xilinx Vivado, expertise in languages like Verilog, VHDL, SystemVerilog, and C or C++ for embedded systems, and knowledge of both analog circuits and digital circuits are essential.
