## Section: Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat & 1. Initiative Overview & 2. College Participation

### Question 1 (Factoid)
What is the name of the initiative launched by the Government of India to enhance unity in diversity across the nation?

**Answer**:
Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat

---
### Question 2 (Factoid)
When was the Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat initiative launched?

**Answer**:
October 31st, 2015

---
### Question 3 (Factoid)
What is the primary goal of the Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat initiative?

**Answer**:
To establish a sustained and structured cultural connection among denizens of different regions of India

---
### Question 4 (Exploratory)
What is the concept behind the Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat initiative?

**Answer**:
State pairing to promote interaction and mutual understanding between people of different states and union territories

---
### Question 5 (Factoid)
Which states are paired under the Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat initiative?

**Answer**:
Tamil Nadu and Jammu and Kashmir

---
### Question 6 (Exploratory)
What are some of the areas where cultural connection is promoted under the Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat initiative?

**Answer**:
Language learning, culture, traditions, music, tourism, cuisine, sports, and the sharing of best practices

---
