## Section: Anti-Ragging Policy at Mohamed Sathak A.J. College of Engineering (**MSAJCE**), Siruseri, Chennai & 1. Punishments and Disciplinary Actions

### Question 1 (Factoid)
What is the Anti-Ragging Policy at Mohamed Sathak A.J. College of Engineering (MSAJCE)?

**Answer**:
The Anti-Ragging Policy at Mohamed Sathak A.J. College of Engineering (MSAJCE) strictly prohibits any indiscipline activities by students that cause or are likely to cause annoyance, hardship, physical or psychological harm, or raise fear or apprehension in any fresher or other student.

---
### Question 2 (Factoid)
What are the consequences of ragging at MSAJCE?

**Answer**:
Students found guilty of ragging at MSAJCE face severe disciplinary actions and punishments, including withholding or withdrawing scholarships, debarment from appearing in tests, examinations, or other evaluation processes, and suspension or expulsion from MSAJCE hostel.

---
### Question 3 (Yes_no)
Is exploiting the services of any student to complete academic tasks a violation of the policy?

**Answer**:
Yes, exploiting the services of any student to complete academic tasks assigned to an individual or group is considered a severe violation of the policy.

---
### Question 4 (Factoid)
What types of physical abuse are prohibited under the policy?

**Answer**:
The policy maintains a zero-tolerance approach toward any physical abuse, encompassing all its variants such as sexual abuse, homosexual assaults, stripping, forcing obscene and lewd acts or gestures, causing bodily harm, or posing any danger to health or person.

---
### Question 5 (Factoid)
What are the punishments for severe ragging violations?

**Answer**:
More severe consequences for ragging violations include immediate suspension or expulsion from MSAJCE hostel, as well as the outright cancellation of their admission to MSAJCE. Depending on the severity of the offense, the administration may rusticate the student from MSAJCE for a period ranging from one to four semesters. In the most extreme cases, the punishment involves permanent expulsion from MSAJCE, along with consequent debarring from admission to any other educational institution for a specified period.

---
### Question 6 (Yes_no)
Is it forbidden to ask any student to perform acts they would not ordinarily do?

**Answer**:
Yes, it is strictly forbidden to ask any student to perform acts they would not ordinarily do, especially those that generate a sense of shame, torment, or embarrassment affecting their physical or psychological well-being.

---
### Question 7 (Procedural)
What is the procedure for dealing with students who engage in ragging?

**Answer**:
The administration reserves the right to withhold or withdraw scholarships, fellowships, and any other associated benefits from the offenders. Guilty individuals may be debarred from appearing in any tests, examinations, or other evaluation processes, and their academic results may be formally withheld.

---
### Question 8 (Factoid)
What are the consequences of debarment from MSAJCE?

**Answer**:
Debarment from MSAJCE may result in the student being unable to represent MSAJCE in any regional, national, or international meets, tournaments, or youth festivals.
