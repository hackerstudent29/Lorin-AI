## Section: Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) & 1. Overview & 2. Objective of IQAC & 3. Strategies & 4. Functions

### Question 1 (Exploratory)
What is the purpose of the Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC)?

**Answer**:
The prime task of the IQAC is to develop a system for conscious, consistent and catalytic improvement in the performance of institutions.

---
### Question 2 (Factoid)
What is the objective of the IQAC?

**Answer**:
The primary objective is to develop a system for conscious, consistent and catalytic action to improve the academic and administrative performance of MSAJCE.

---
### Question 3 (Exploratory)
What are the strategies of the IQAC?

**Answer**:
The IQAC shall evolve mechanisms and procedures for ensuring timely, efficient and progressive performance of academic, administrative and financial tasks.

---
### Question 4 (Exploratory)
What are the functions of the IQAC?

**Answer**:
The functions include the development and application of quality benchmarks for the various academic and administrative activities of MSAJCE, and the dissemination of information on the various quality parameters of higher education.

---
### Question 5 (Yes_no)
Is the IQAC a hierarchical structure or a recordkeeping exercise?

**Answer**:
It will not be yet another hierarchical structure or recordkeeping exercise in MSAJCE, rather it will be a facilitative and participative voluntary system of MSAJCE.

---
### Question 6 (Exploratory)
What is the role of the IQAC in the post-accreditation phase?

**Answer**:
The IQAC will make a significant and meaningful contribution in the post-accreditation phase of institutions. During the post-accreditation period, the IQAC will channelise the efforts and measures of an institution towards academic excellence.

---
### Question 7 (Exploratory)
What is the potential of the IQAC?

**Answer**:
The IQAC has the potential to become a vehicle for ushering in quality by working out intervention strategies to remove deficiencies and enhance quality.

---
### Question 8 (Exploratory)
What is the focus of the IQAC on academic and research programmes?

**Answer**:
The IQAC will focus on the relevance and quality of academic and research programmes, as well as equitable access to and affordability of academic programmes for various sections of society.

---
### Question 9 (Exploratory)
What is the role of the IQAC in research sharing and networking?

**Answer**:
The IQAC is responsible for the optimization and integration of modern methods of teaching and learning, the credibility of evaluation procedures, and ensuring the adequacy, maintenance and functioning of the support structure and services. It also focuses on research sharing and networking with other institutions in India and abroad.

---
### Question 10 (Exploratory)
What is the purpose of the Annual Quality Assurance Report (AQAR)?

**Answer**:
The IQAC is responsible for the preparation of the Annual Quality Assurance Report (AQAR) to be submitted to NAAC based on the quality parameters.

---
