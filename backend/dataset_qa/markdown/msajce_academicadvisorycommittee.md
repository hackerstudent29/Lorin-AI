## Section: Academic Advisory Committee & 1. Committee Members

### Question 1 (Factoid)
Who is the Chairman of the Academic Advisory Committee at MSAJCE?

**Answer**:
Dr. K.S. Srinivasan, the Principal of MSAJCE in Chennai, serves as the Chairman of the Academic Advisory Committee.

---
### Question 2 (Factoid)
What is the role of Dr. K. Malathi in the Academic Advisory Committee?

**Answer**:
Dr. K. Malathi, Professor of Electronics and Communication Engineering at Anna University in Chennai, serves as an Advisor in the Academic Advisory Committee.

---
### Question 3 (Factoid)
Who are the institutional heads from MSAJCE that serve as Members of the Academic Advisory Committee?

**Answer**:
The institutional heads from MSAJCE that serve as Members of the committee include the Head of Administration, the Head of Academics, the Head of Research, the Head of IQAC, the Head of Student Affairs, the Head of the Exam Cell, the Head of Placement, and the Head of Admission.

The committee includes the Principal of MSAJCE as the Chairman, several esteemed Advisors from various institutions and industries, and several institutional heads from MSAJCE as Members.
