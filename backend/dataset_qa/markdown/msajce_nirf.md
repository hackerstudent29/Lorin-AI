### Question 1 (Factoid)
What is the National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF)?

**Answer**:
The National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF) is a methodology adopted by the Ministry of Education, Government of India, to rank institutions of higher education across the country.

---
### Question 2 (Factoid)
In which categories does MSAJCE participate in NIRF rankings?

**Answer**:
MSAJCE participates in NIRF rankings annually and submits data across three categories, which are Engineering, Overall, and Innovation.
