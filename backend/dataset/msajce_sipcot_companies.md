# Companies and Infrastructure in SIPCOT IT Park, Siruseri

The **SIPCOT IT Park** in **Siruseri**, where **Mohamed Sathak A.J. College of Engineering (MSAJCE)** is located, is predominantly an **IT and ITES** hub. It also houses significant **data center** infrastructure and a few specialized **electronics/defense** manufacturing units. While the park's mandate focuses on technology, it does not typically host traditional non-IT manufacturing (like textiles or heavy machinery), which are located in other SIPCOT complexes (e.g., Sriperumbudur or Oragadam).

Here is the comprehensive list of companies, brands, and infrastructure operating within the park as of 2026:

## Major IT & Software Services
These companies occupy the largest land parcels and form the core of the park:

- **Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)**: The largest occupant with over 70 acres and multiple buildings focused on IT services and R&D.
- **Cognizant Technology Solutions (CTS)**: Operates a massive campus (Plots B-40 to B-44) for digital solutions and BPO.
- **Capgemini**: Located in Plot H-6, focusing on engineering and digital transformation.
- **HCL Technologies**: Maintains a substantial campus for software development and infrastructure management.
- **Hexaware Technologies**: One of the early major tenants with a large dedicated facility.
- **Infosys**: Operates a significant development center within the park.
- **Atos** (formerly **Atos Syntel**): Continues operations following the acquisition of Syntel.
- **Aspire Systems**: Headquartered in the park (Plot 1/D-1), specializing in engineering and R&D services.
- **Polaris Software Lab** (part of **LTIMindtree**): A key early tenant focused on banking and financial software.
- **CareVoyant Technologies**: Located in Plot 26/A-39, specializing in home health software.
- **Placka Information Technologies**: Provides IT solutions from Plot 6/A-12.

## Data Centers & Digital Infrastructure
Siruseri has emerged as a critical data center hub with recent high-profile inaugurations:

- **Sify Technologies**: Inaugurated South India’s largest **AI-ready data center** campus in April 2025. The facility spans multiple towers with a total investment exceeding ₹10,000 crore, offering 130 MW capacity.
- **Equinix**: Opened its first Chennai International Business Exchange (**IBX**) data center (CN1) in September 2025. The ₹574 crore facility covers six acres, initially housing 800 cabinets (scalable to 4,250) with liquid cooling for AI workloads.

## Electronics, Defense & Specialized Manufacturing
While rare in this specific IT park, some high-tech manufacturing and defense electronics units are present:

- **Data Patterns (India) Limited**: Located at Plot No. H-9, this is a key **defense and aerospace** electronics manufacturer. Unlike typical IT firms, it engages in the design and production of advanced electronic subsystems for defense platforms.
- **FT8012 (Fidelity Technologies)**: Operates an innovation center within the park, often associated with financial technology hardware and specialized software solutions.

## Other Notable Entities
- **ETA Technopark**: A commercial technology complex located immediately adjacent/within the Siruseri stretch, often grouped with the SIPCOT ecosystem, housing various smaller tech firms and startups.
- **Polaris Financial Technologies**: Often listed separately due to its specific focus on financial software products before its merger into LTIMindtree.

> **Note on Non-IT Companies:** The SIPCOT IT Park in Siruseri is zoned specifically for **IT, ITES, Electronics, and Data Centers**. You will not find traditional non-IT brands (e.g., consumer goods, automotive assembly, textiles) here; those are located in nearby SIPCOT parks like **Sriperumbudur** (automotive) or **Oragadam** (manufacturing). The "non-IT" presence in Siruseri is limited to **defense electronics** (Data Patterns) and **facility management/support services** required to run the large campuses.
