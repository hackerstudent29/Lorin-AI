# Placement Cell Overview, Mission, Objectives, and Placement Committee at MSAJCE

The Placement Cell at Mohamed Sathak A.J. College of Engineering (MSAJCE), located in Siruseri IT Park, Chennai, provides career guidance, industry skill development, and campus recruitment opportunities for all engineering and technology disciplines. The primary objectives of the Placement Cell include arranging campus recruitment drives, offering aptitude and soft skill training, organizing technical certifications, conducting mock interview sessions, facilitating student internships, arranging industrial visits, and encouraging entrepreneurship. The official Placement Cell webpage is accessible at https://www.msajce-edu.in/placement.php.
