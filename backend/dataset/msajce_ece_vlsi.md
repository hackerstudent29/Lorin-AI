# Electronics Engineering with Specialization in VLSI Design and Technology

## 1. Overview
- The B.Tech in Electronics Engineering with Specialization in VLSI Design and Technology addresses the exponential demand for integrated chips and sensors driven by the proliferation of the digital economy and consumer electronics.
- Meeting this burgeoning domestic demand is challenging as India currently imports integrated chips in billions of US dollars.
- The post-COVID global semiconductor market has grown exponentially and is expected to reach one trillion dollars by 2030, driven by technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, Electric Vehicles, 5G Networks, the Internet of Things, and Health Care.
- Realization of these technologies depends on the production of silicon chips, leading to the establishment of many chip-making industries in research and development, design, manufacturing, and testing to fill the gap in the chip supply chain.
- Overall semiconductor import has surpassed that of gold, standing second only to crude oil, and the deficit is expected to rise further from a long-term economic perspective.
- Hence, the existing gap must be bridged by realizing indigenous manufacturing of integrated chips.
- Most of the tape-out is currently being siliconized in foreign countries since India has only a single Integrated Design to Manufacturing pilot line at the Semi-Conductor Laboratory based on the 180nm Node.
- Recently, the Government of India has given impetus to the Electronics System Design and Manufacturing policy for full-flow custom design solutions.
- Foundries and companies are encouraged to set up chip manufacturing units under the Indian Semiconductor Mission to build vibrant ecosystems of semiconductor and display systems. A common concern associated with setting up and running a commercial fab in India is the availability of trained manpower required to run the system.
- A serious crunch is observed in the trained manpower in every aspect of the semiconductor ecosystem in the country.
- Various schemes and policies are framed by the Government of India to encourage start-ups, MSMEs, industries, and large business houses, including the Chips to Startup programme of the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology.
- It is estimated that the total manpower requirement over the next five to seven years will be around 85,000 across undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral levels in the fields of VLSI Technology, Semiconductor Manufacturing, Electronics, Electrical, Mechanical, and Chemical Engineering.
- The Government of India announced a Semiconductor scheme with a massive outlay to fulfill the Aatma Nirbhar Bharath vision and contribute significantly to the global semiconductor market.
- **MSAJCE** is dedicated to becoming a part of this endeavor to create the human resources required for the semiconductor industry in the upcoming years by providing trained engineers through the National Semiconductor Mission.
- The outcome of this program is to improve the world through transformative education and impactful research by producing outstanding graduates who are knowledgeable, creative, and passionate about building a vibrant semiconductor and display ecosystem to enable India’s emergence as a global hub for electronics manufacturing and design.
- B.Tech in Electronics Engineering with Specialization in VLSI Design and Technology is offered in the Faculty of Electronics and Communication Engineering.
- VLSI is the process of designing and manufacturing integrated circuits.
- The VLSI design curriculum emphasizes the key aspects of software and hardware design and development of VLSI applications such as device modeling, RF circuit design, testing and diagnosis of VLSI circuits, VLSI signal processing, chip packaging, system design verification, and system on chip.
- VLSI Design and its applications are found in areas like signal processing, image processing, networks and communication applications, space applications, and quantum computing.
- This programme aims to cater to the exponential rise in demand for a semiconductor talent pool in India and across the globe, preparing market-ready engineers for the semiconductor technology and electronics industries.
- The course is structured in line with the AICTE model curriculum and covers a range of topics from semiconductor device fundamentals, analog and digital circuits to VLSI circuits and design, IC design and technology, semiconductor equipment operation, manufacturing, assembly, testing and packaging, microcontrollers, embedded systems, algorithms in design automation, logic synthesis, testability, device and process simulation, and physical verification flows.
- It also includes the study and usage of Electronic Design Automation tools and programming and scripting languages for semiconductor design, synthesis, simulation, fabrication, and testing.
- The main objective of providing this course is to offer varying roles of employment featuring attractive salary incentives and solid professional growth, especially with the establishment of India’s largest semiconductor fab plant at Mysuru.
- It also prepares students to pursue higher education in reputed universities in India and abroad, as VLSI is in great demand in companies like Intel, IBM, Cadence, Synopsis, and Mentor Graphics.
- Furthermore, it provides insights into market trends and wider opportunities to take up projects under the Make in India and Digital India schemes, encouraging entrepreneurial endeavors in creating innovative products based on VLSI chipsets.

## 2. Career Prospects
- There are numerous job opportunities for VLSI engineers in the Semiconductor industry.
- These opportunities include roles as Design Engineers, Verification Engineers, CAD Engineers, Application Engineers, Analog Circuit Designers, DFT Engineers, FPGA Verification Engineers, EDA Tool Validation Engineers, and VLSI Micro-architecture Engineers among many others.
- On successful completion of the course, graduates are recruited in Fabless design companies, Display Fabs, Semiconductor Fabs, Fabs for Compound Semiconductors, Silicon Photonics, Sensors, Discrete Semiconductors, and Semiconductor Assembly, Testing, Marking, and Packaging facilities.
- The starting salary in a VLSI company is highly competitive, and core multinational companies offer substantial average annual salaries for beginners.
- Prominent companies recruiting VLSI graduates include Intel Corporation, Global Foundries, Micron Technologies, Marvell Semiconductor, ARM, AMD, NVIDIA, Infineon Technologies, MediaTek, NXP Semiconductors, TSMC, Qualcomm, Philips, Samsung, Siemens, IBM, Bosch, Wipro, Tata Elxsi, Dell, Apex Semiconductor, Free Scale Semiconductor, Texas Instruments, Synopsis, Cadence, and Microchip.
- Many government public sector undertakings also recruit VLSI engineers, such as SCL, ISRO, DRDO, HAL, and BEL.

## 3. Programme
- The program offered is a B.Tech in Electronics Engineering with Specialization in VLSI Design and Technology.
- The duration is four years for regular students and three years for lateral entry students, comprising eight and six semesters, respectively.
- The total intake is 30 seats, divided equally with 15 for the government quota and 15 for the management quota.
- Eligibility for the program requires completion of the 10+2 system of education, and students must have secured a pass in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics in their qualifying examination.
- The curriculum and syllabus follow the 2021 Regulation.
- Opportunities are vast in public sector undertakings and the government sector.
- The scope for higher studies includes pursuing an M.E., M.Tech, or M.B.A.

## 9. Job Profiles

### VLSI Design Employment Opportunity Forecast by 2025 - 2030 in India
- The VLSI Design industry in India is poised for substantial growth over the next five years, driven by advancements in Semiconductor technology, 5G and 6G networks, Automotive electronics, and the Internet of Things.

### Key Industry Drivers
- The growth of the semiconductor industry is a primary driver, as India increases its focus on domestic semiconductor manufacturing, propelled by the National Policy on Electronics and manufacturing plans from both government and private entities.
- Large-scale investments by companies like Intel, TSMC, Samsung, and Global Foundries require skilled VLSI engineers for designing, testing, and validating chips.
- With the roll-out of 5G networks and planning for 6G, there is a massive demand for VLSI design for communication chips, RF components, and signal processing units, creating opportunities for professionals skilled in RFIC, mixed-signal design, and high-speed digital circuit design.
- The rise of Electric Vehicles and Advanced Driver Assistance Systems requires VLSI design for power management, sensors, and control systems, leading to a specialized job market for autonomous driving chips and energy-efficient electronics.
- As India moves towards the Smart India initiative with IoT-based applications, the demand for low-power system-on-chip designs grows for wearables, health devices, and home automation systems. The advent of AI-driven hardware accelerators for machine learning also drives the need for specialized skills in hardware design for AI, edge computing, and neural networks.

### Sector-Wise Job Growth Projections and Opportunities
- Semiconductor Design is projected to see high growth, hiring heavily for Front-end VLSI design, SoC architecture, and physical design.
- Telecommunication for 5G and 6G networks seeks expertise in RFIC design, signal processing, and analog-digital integration.
- Automotive Electronics focuses on hiring for ADAS chips, EV power management, and sensor design.
- IoT and Consumer Electronics look for talent in low-power design, system-on-chip design, and embedded systems. AI and ML Hardware projects the highest growth, seeking professionals for AI accelerators, FPGAs, GPUs, and edge computing hardware.
- Test and Verification roles also see steady growth, demanding verification engineers for automated test benches and design for testing.
- Key skills in demand include proficiency in design tools such as Cadence, Mentor Graphics, Synopsys, and Xilinx Vivado.
- Expertise in languages like Verilog, VHDL, SystemVerilog, and C or C++ for embedded systems is critical.
- Knowledge of both analog circuits like transistors and op-amps, and digital circuits like logic gates and multiplexers is essential.
- Experience in functional and formal verification, Design Verification Test, and Universal Verification Methodology is highly valued.
- Skills in RF circuit design, signal processing, low-power design, and hardware design for AI accelerators are also crucial.

### Key Government and Industry Initiatives
- The National Semiconductor Mission generates significant jobs in VLSI design and fabrication.
- The Production-Linked Incentive scheme aims to increase domestic manufacturing and assembly of electronics, fostering job creation.
- The push for Smart Cities and Digital India increases reliance on IoT and sensor networks, driving demand in consumer electronics and automotive sectors.
- The shift toward Electric Vehicles and ADAS increases the need for VLSI design in power electronics and control systems.

### Employment Forecast Summary
- VLSI Design in India will experience exceptional annual growth in the next five years, fueled by demand in semiconductor manufacturing, telecommunications, IoT, automotive, and AI hardware sectors.
- The focus on domestic semiconductor fabrication, 5G and 6G networks, AI chip design, and electric vehicle electronics will open up numerous roles for VLSI engineers, especially those with expertise in RF, analog and mixed-signal design, embedded systems, and verification.
