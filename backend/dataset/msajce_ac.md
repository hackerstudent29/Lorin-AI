The 2026-2027 academic year at Mohamed Sathak A.J. College of Engineering (MSAJCE), Siruseri, Chennai, commenced for II, III, and V year students on July 15, 2026. At MSAJCE, students are required to pay their tuition, training, hostel, and transport fees for the academic year on or before July 25, 2026. Maintaining a minimum of 80% attendance is strictly mandatory for students at MSAJCE to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examinations, as per Anna University and MSAJCE regulations. Internal assessment marks, which account for 40 marks, and attendance at MSAJCE are earned through consistent academic performance and are not granted automatically to students. Parent-Teacher Meetings at MSAJCE are scheduled during the first week of every month, between the 1st and the 5th, and attendance at these meetings is mandatory for parents, either through online mode or by meeting the respective Head of the Department (HOD) or Principal in person at least once per semester. For the most specific or updated dates regarding internal assessment tests (IAT), holidays, and end-semester examinations, students and parents are advised to check the College Notice Board, contact their respective Department Coordinator, or visit the official MSAJCE website at https://www.msajce-edu.in.
