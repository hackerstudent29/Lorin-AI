# Technology Centres

## 1. Overview
- In pursuit of **MSAJCE** vision and mission to make the students deployable and equip them with the upcoming emerging industrial demands, various Technology Centres to educate, train and up-skill students in niche technologies were established in the year 2019-2020.
- The courses and training are designed based on the recommendations of AICTE and India Skill Report 2020.
- The Technology Centers are established with state of art facility, providing an ambient learning environment for research and innovation.
- Each centre is collaborated with industry or government organizations to provide certifications, onsite or offsite projects and internships.
- The list of industries include Automation Anywhere, Godrej Inc, Ford, Palo Alto systems, Amazon Web Services, National Instruments, CISCO Academy, Altair, Open wave computing, Levergent Technologies, and Propeller Technologies.

## 2. MECH and CIVIL Technology Centres
- The Mechanical and Civil Engineering departments host several specialized skill development centres.
- These include the Centre for 3D Printing and Additive Manufacturing, which provides training on modern manufacturing techniques.
- There is also the Centre for Computer-Aided Engineering, along with the Centre for Industrial Robotics.
- Additionally, students can access the Centre for Non-Destructive Testing and the Centre for Building Information Modelling.

## 3. ECE and EEE Technology Centres
- The Electronics and Communication Engineering, as well as Electrical and Electronics Engineering departments, operate multiple technology centres.
- These feature the Centre for Embedded and IOT, and the Centre for UAV and Drone Technology.
- The Godrej Disha Skill Development Centre and the Centre for Bosch Industry Institute Collaboration offer specialized industry-aligned training.
- Other key facilities include the Centre for E-Mobility, the Centre for Renewable Energy, and the APJ Abdul Kalam Innovation Centre.

## 4. CSE and IT Technology Centres
- The Computer Science and Engineering, along with Information Technology departments, maintain a variety of advanced training centres.
- These consist of the Centre for Bot Lab and Robotics Process Automation, the Centre for Cisco Networking Academy, and the Centre for Code Tantra Coding Learning Centre.
- Students can also explore the Centre for GAMING AND AR or VR, the Centre for Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Centre, the Centre for Block Chain Technology, and the Centre for Mobile and Web Application Development.
