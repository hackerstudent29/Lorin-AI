# ECE-VLSI

## Overview

The B.Tech in Electronics Engineering with Specialization in VLSI Design & Technology is a highly sought-after program, driven by the rapid growth of the digital economy and consumer electronics. This growth has led to an exponential increase in the demand for integrated chips and sensors, posing a significant challenge for India as it struggles to meet this demand domestically. Currently, the country relies heavily on imports, with billions of US dollars spent on integrated chips each year.

The global semiconductor market has experienced tremendous growth, with a value of $590 billion in 2021 and an expected growth to $1 trillion by 2030. This growth is largely driven by emerging technologies such as Artificial Intelligence (AI), Electric Vehicle (EV), 5G Networks, Internet of Things (IoT), and Health Care, all of which rely on the production of silicon chips for their hardware requirements. As a result, numerous chip-making industries have been established to address the gap in the chip supply chain, focusing on research and development, design, manufacturing, and testing.

The significance of the semiconductor industry is underscored by the fact that the overall import of semiconductors has surpassed that of gold, ranking second only to crude oil. From a long-term economic perspective, this deficit is expected to rise further, emphasizing the need to bridge the existing gap by promoting indigenous manufacturing of integrated chips.

The majority of tape-out processes in India are currently being outsourced to foreign countries due to the limited availability of domestic facilities, with the country having only a single Integrated Design to Manufacturing pilot line at the Semi-Conductor Laboratory in Chandigarh, which operates on a 180nm Node.

In recent times, the Government of India has taken significant steps to boost the Electronics System Design & Manufacturing policy, focusing on providing full-flow and custom design solutions that encompass both front-end and back-end processes. As part of this initiative, foundries and companies are being encouraged to establish chip manufacturing units under the Indian Semiconductor Mission, with the ultimate goal of creating a thriving ecosystem for semiconductor and display systems.

However, one of the primary concerns associated with setting up and operating a commercial fabrication unit in India is the availability of skilled personnel required to manage the system effectively.

The semiconductor ecosystem in India is currently facing a significant shortage of skilled manpower, a challenge that affects various aspects of the industry. In response to this issue, the Government of India has introduced several schemes and policies aimed at promoting start-ups, Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs), industries, and large business houses. One notable initiative is the Chips to Startup (C2S) programme launched by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (Meity), which is part of the Government of India's efforts to boost the sector.


According to estimates, the industry will require a substantial workforce of around 85,000 professionals over the next five to seven years, spanning various levels of expertise, including B.Tech, M.Tech, and Ph.D. holders in specialized fields such as VLSI Technology, Semiconductor Manufacturing, Electronics, Electrical, Mechanical, and Chemical Engineering. In a significant move to address this need and contribute to the country's goal of achieving Aatma Nirbhar Bharath, the Government of India announced a Semiconductor scheme in December 2021, with a substantial outlay of Rs 76,000 Crore, aimed at making a significant impact on the global semiconductor market.

The Government of India has embarked on an ambitious initiative, the National Semiconductor Mission (NSM), with the goal of establishing a thriving Semiconductor ecosystem within the country. Through this program, MSAJCE aims to make a profound impact on the world by providing transformative education and conducting impactful research. The ultimate objective is to produce exceptional graduates who possess a deep understanding of the field, think creatively, and are passionate about building a vibrant semiconductor and display ecosystem. By achieving this goal, India will be poised to emerge as a global hub for electronics manufacturing and design, further solidifying its position on the world stage.

The Faculty of Electronics and Communication Engineering at Mohamed Sathak A.J. College of Engineering offers a B.Tech in Electronics Engineering with a specialization in VLSI Design and Technology. This specialization focuses on the process of designing and manufacturing integrated circuits, commonly referred to as ICs. The VLSI design curriculum places a strong emphasis on the key aspects of both software and hardware design and development of VLSI applications.

These applications encompass a wide range of areas, including device modeling, RF circuit design, testing and diagnosis of VLSI circuits, VLSI signal processing, chip packaging, system design verification, and SOC. The applications of VLSI design are diverse and can be found in various fields such as signal processing, image processing, networks and communication applications, space applications, and even quantum computing.

The primary objective of this program is to address the rapidly growing demand for semiconductor talent in India and globally. By providing students with a comprehensive education in semiconductor technology and electronics industries, the program aims to produce market-ready engineers who are equipped to meet the needs of the industry.

Additionally, the course delves into Microcontrollers, Embedded systems, Algorithms in Design Automation, Logic Synthesis, Testability, and Design for Testability, as well as Device and Process simulation of semiconductor devices and Physical Verification Flows.

The curriculum also places a strong emphasis on the study and usage of Electronic Design Automation (EDA) tools, as well as programming and scripting languages that are essential for semiconductor design, synthesis, simulation, fabrication, and testing. By incorporating these aspects, the VLSI Design course aims to cater to the ever-changing demands of the VLSI industry and the needs of society through research.
