# ECE

## Overview

The Electronics and Communication Engineering program at Mohamed Sathak A.J. College of Engineering is designed to equip students with the knowledge and skills to apply electronics concepts and principles to transfer information from one place to another. By utilizing science and mathematical concepts to solve practical problems, students in this program can create innovative products that make human life more comfortable. The rapid development and penetration of Information and Communication Technology (ICT) have revolutionized various fields, including healthcare, automation, remote sensing, and advanced communication.

The B.E. in Electronics and Communication Engineering is a four-year regular program or a three-year lateral entry program, consisting of eight semesters for regular students and six semesters for lateral entry students. The program has a total intake of 60 students, divided equally between government and management seats. To be eligible for this program, students must have completed the 10+2 system of education and secured a pass in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics in their qualifying examination.

Upon completing the program, students can pursue various career opportunities in both hardware and software industries, including roles such as system engineers, research and development engineers, academicians, software engineers, network engineers, and maintenance engineers. Some of the companies that have hired students from this program include Aagree Equipments Pvt. Ltd., Apps Daily Solution, O M Innovation Services, Road Map IT Solution, and Infoview. Additionally, students who complete this program can also pursue higher studies in their field of interest.

The department has also established several Programme Educational Objectives (PEOs) to guide its efforts. PEO1 emphasizes the importance of preparing graduates to design Electronics and Communication components and systems with creativity and sustainability. PEO2 focuses on equipping graduates with the skills to use modern tools for critical problem-solving and analyzing industrial and societal requirements.
