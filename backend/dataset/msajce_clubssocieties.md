# CLUBSSOCIETIES

## 1. SPORTS CLUB
- The Sports Club at Mohamed Sathak A.J. College of Engineering plays a vital role in maintaining records of sports and games events attended by students within **MSAJCE**, university, and at regional, state, and national levels, while also documenting their achievements and awards.
- One of the key responsibilities of the club is to submit an annual report on sports events, budget allocations, and expenditures during the year.
- Furthermore, the club organizes sports events that cater to the needs of students and rewards their achievements with trophies and medals.
- The Sports Club has a multifaceted objective that encompasses various aspects of personal growth and development.
- Its primary aim is to build up sportsmanship among students, while also fostering intimate friendships that can last a lifetime.
- Additionally, the club seeks to reduce stress and improve health, as well as enhance skills and employability.
- It also supports the development of a positive attitude, self-confidence, courage, and patience among students.
- By participating in sports events, students get opportunities to closely observe the community and gain experience of human nature in relation to their environment.
- Moreover, the club provides a platform for students to interact with people from diverse backgrounds and walks of life.
- The vision of the Sports Club is to encourage and motivate students to participate in various sports activities at both inter and intra college levels.
- The Sports Club at Mohamed Sathak A.J. College of Engineering organizes a variety of games and activities that foster teamwork and camaraderie among students.
- Outdoor games are a significant part of the club's offerings, with popular sports like Cricket, Football, Volleyball, Basketball, and Rugby being played on **MSAJCE** grounds.
- Additionally, students can also participate in Badminton and kho kho, as well as Throughball, which provides a unique opportunity for students to engage in a fun and fast-paced game.
- Indoor games are also an essential part of the Sports Club, with students having access to facilities for Carrom, Table tennis, and Chess.
- These games not only provide a platform for students to showcase their skills but also help them develop strategic thinking and problem-solving abilities.

## 3. SCIENCE CLUB
- The Science Club at Mohamed Sathak A.J. College of Engineering was established with the primary objective of inviting renowned scientists to share their experiences and inspire students to acquire scientific knowledge.
- By designing engaging activities, the club aims to introduce students to exciting science and technology projects that foster a deeper understanding of the scientific world.
- The Science Club is an inclusive organization, open to all students, and comprises a group of enthusiastic individuals who are eager to explore the various branches of engineering, mathematics, and science.
- As one of the largest clubs in **MSAJCE**, it is divided into three distinct sections: Physics, Chemistry, and General.
- The club's purpose is multifaceted, seeking to promote interest, understanding, and knowledge of science among college students and the local community.
- One of the club's primary goals is to stimulate engineers' interest in learning science, thereby enhancing their academic achievement in science subjects.
- Through its activities, the club also aims to teach students how to apply their scientific knowledge in daily life, guiding them to develop independent, logical, and creative minds.
- To achieve this, the club organizes a variety of activities, including experiments in making electronic components, invisible ink, electroplating, biodiesels, fire writing, and creating venation of leaves.
- To keep students informed, the club displays the latest news and day-to-day information about science on the notice board, ensuring that all engineers have access to the latest updates and developments in the scientific world.
- The Science Club at Mohamed Sathak A.J. College of Engineering operates under a set of guiding principles that serve as a foundation for developing engaging activities.
- These principles govern the design of the activities, which are centered around five key objectives.
- One of the primary activities offered by the Science Club is the Investigator program, which provides students with the opportunity to tackle real-world, science-based problems within a given scenario.
- This program allows students to work independently or in pairs/small groups, engaging in hands-on science experiments and activities that foster critical thinking and discussion about scientific concepts.
- Through the Investigator program, students are encouraged to share their findings using various forms of media, promoting effective communication and presentation skills.
- In addition to the Investigator program, the Science Club organizes a range of activities aimed at enhancing students' knowledge and understanding of science.
- These activities include conducting seminars, workshops, exhibitions, and quiz competitions, all of which are designed to stimulate students' interest in the field of science.
- Furthermore, the Science Club arranges monthly lectures to provide students with a deeper understanding of scientific concepts and principles.
- The Science Club also promotes the use of information technology in learning science, recognizing the importance of digital tools in modern scientific inquiry.
- By encouraging active participation in Science Club activities, students are able to engage with their peers and develop a sense of community and collaboration.
- The club also facilitates monthly discussions on recent innovations in the field of science, allowing students to stay up-to-date with the latest developments and advancements.
- To create a visually engaging learning environment, the Science Club encourages students to decorate the walls of classrooms, libraries, and laboratories with scientific pictures and charts prepared by the students themselves.

## 4. தமிழ் மன்றம்
- The Tamil Mandram, or Tamil Association, is an entity that strives to promote the eternal and unchanging nature of the Tamil language.
- Its primary objective is to bring about a transformation in the younger generation of Tamils, as well as in society at large, to ensure the continued relevance and importance of Tamil.
- As the saying goes, "Tamil, the sweetest of languages, must live on and thrive forever!" and "May Tamil flourish everywhere!" Our mission is to rekindle the full potential of young Tamils and to instill in them a sense of pride and responsibility towards their mother tongue.
- The Tamil Mandram aims to achieve several goals, including fostering a love for Tamil among students, promoting knowledge of the language, and encouraging its use in everyday life.
- Additionally, the association seeks to introduce young people to the rich cultural heritage and traditions of ancient Tamil society, as well as to promote the use of Tamil in modern technology.
- To achieve these objectives, the Tamil Mandram organizes various events and activities, including cultural programs, competitions, and seminars, which are designed to engage students and promote their interest in the language.
- Some of the notable events organized by the Tamil Mandram include "Irumugam Oru Agam," "Pesum Padam," "Aadu Puli," "Kaivanna Kaviyam," "Dentkottai," "Vidukathai Vilayattu," "Prasangathin Thalaivan," and "Tamil Korvai." These events provide a platform for students to showcase their talents, develop their language skills, and learn about the rich cultural heritage of Tamil Nadu.

## 5. CONDING CLUB
- In today's rapidly changing world, programming abilities have become essential tools that can be applied to and integrated into various disciplines and domains.
- Recognizing this, the Coding Club at Mohamed Sathak A.J. College of Engineering aims to promote coding skills among students through a range of activities.
- To achieve this goal, the club plans to organize more coding contests to reach out to all students on campus, providing them with opportunities to develop their coding skills.
- Additionally, the club intends to conduct workshops on various computer science topics such as web development, application development, machine learning, and IoT, among others.
- The club also aims to participate in open source initiatives, create websites and applications, and offer technical assistance to other clubs and groups on campus.
- Furthermore, the club seeks to represent **MSAJCE** at intercollegiate competitions, where students can showcase their coding skills and compete with their peers from other institutions.
- The club also encourages its members to participate in online coding events such as ACM-ICPC, Google Code Jam, and Google Summer of Code, which provide a platform for students to demonstrate their coding abilities and learn from others.
- To identify new members who are enthusiastic about coding, the club holds a college-wide coding competition that emphasizes reasoning, analysis, and problem-solving abilities in addition to coding skills.
- The club meets once a week to discuss and work on various initiatives, where all members collaborate to achieve a shared goal.
- Through the club, workshops and technical discussions are conducted on various competitions and computer science topics, providing members with opportunities to learn and grow.
- The club also organizes lecture sessions where computer science principles are taught, benefiting its members.
- Moreover, the club hosts seminars and workshops conducted by seniors and former members, who share their experiences and provide guidance on how to prepare for internships and job placements.

## 6. ROBOTIC CLUB

- By participating in the club, students have the opportunity to design and construct a robot that can compete against opponents in various challenges, with the ultimate goal of winning the robotics tournament.
- The primary aim of the Robotics Club is to instill in its members the habit of "thinking technologically," enabling them to approach problems and challenges with a technological mindset.
- To achieve this, the club has set several objectives, including encouraging young people to become the driving force behind the robotics industry.
- The club also aims to provide students with the opportunity to creatively apply information and observe how systems interact with their environment and each other.
- Through its various activities, the Robotics Club seeks to equip students with the professional engineering skills that will benefit them in their future careers.

## 7. ENERGY & ECO CLUB

- By fostering a culture of energy conservation, the club helps students develop habits that contribute to addressing the ongoing energy crisis.
- The Energy Club's efforts not only focus on disseminating information about energy efficiency but also on applying these principles in daily life, whether at home or in institutions.

- By empowering students to share this responsibility, the Eco Club inspires a sense of ownership and action among its members, promoting a culture of environmental stewardship.
