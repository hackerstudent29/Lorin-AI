# Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC)

## 1. Overview
- In pursuance of the National Action Plan of the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC), Bangalore, for performance evaluation, assessment and accreditation and quality up gradation of institutions of higher education, the NAAC proposes that every accredited institution should establish an Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) as a post-accreditation quality sustenance measure.
- Since quality enhancement is a continuous process, the IQAC will become a part of an institution’s system and work towards realizing the goals of quality enhancement and sustenance.
- The prime task of the IQAC is to develop a system for conscious, consistent and catalytic improvement in the performance of institutions.
- The IQAC will make a significant and meaningful contribution in the post-accreditation phase of institutions.
- During the post-accreditation period, the IQAC will channelise the efforts and measures of an institution towards academic excellence.
- The guidelines provided will facilitate **MSAJCE** in the creation and operation of the Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC).
- The work of the IQAC is the first step towards the internalization and institutionalization of quality enhancement.
- Its success depends upon the sense of belongingness and participation it can inculcate in all the constituents of **MSAJCE**.
- It will not be yet another hierarchical structure or recordkeeping exercise in **MSAJCE**, rather it will be a facilitative and participative voluntary system of **MSAJCE**.
- The IQAC has the potential to become a vehicle for ushering in quality by working out intervention strategies to remove deficiencies and enhance quality.
- Quality circles in industries operate on similar lines.

## 2. Objective of IQAC
- The primary objective is to develop a system for conscious, consistent and catalytic action to improve the academic and administrative performance of **MSAJCE**.
- It also aims to promote measures for the institutional functioning towards quality enhancement through internalization of quality culture and institutionalization of best practices.

## 3. Strategies
- The IQAC shall evolve mechanisms and procedures for ensuring timely, efficient and progressive performance of academic, administrative and financial tasks.
- It will focus on the relevance and quality of academic and research programmes, as well as equitable access to and affordability of academic programmes for various sections of society.
- The IQAC is responsible for the optimization and integration of modern methods of teaching and learning, the credibility of evaluation procedures, and ensuring the adequacy, maintenance and functioning of the support structure and services.
- It also focuses on research sharing and networking with other institutions in India and abroad.

## 4. Functions
- The functions include the development and application of quality benchmarks for the various academic and administrative activities of **MSAJCE**, and the dissemination of information on the various quality parameters of higher education.
- The IQAC is responsible for the organization of workshops and seminars on quality related themes and the promotion of quality circles.
- It takes charge of the documentation of the various programmes and activities leading to quality improvement, acting as a nodal agency of **MSAJCE** for quality-related activities, and the preparation of the Annual Quality Assurance Report (AQAR) to be submitted to NAAC based on the quality parameters.

## 5. Benefits
- The IQAC will facilitate a heightened level of clarity and focus in institutional functioning towards quality enhancement and facilitate internalization of the quality culture for NAAC for Quality and Excellence in Higher Education.
- It contributes to the enhancement and integration among the various activities of **MSAJCE** and institutionalizes many good practices.
- The cell provides a sound basis for decision making to improve institutional functioning, acts as a change agent in **MSAJCE**, and leads to better internal communication.

## 6. The Role of Coordinator
- The role of the coordinator of the IQAC is crucial in ensuring the effective functioning of all the members.
- The coordinator of the IQAC may be a senior person with expertise in quality aspects.
- The coordinator may be a full-time functionary or a senior academic entrusted with the IQAC as an additional responsibility.
- Secretarial assistance may be facilitated by the administration.
- It is preferable that the coordinator may have a sound knowledge about computers, various functions, and usage for effective communication.

## 7. Operational Features of the IQAC
- Quality assurance is a by-product of ongoing efforts to define the objectives of an institution, to have a work plan to achieve them and to specify the checks and balances to evaluate the degree to which each of the tasks is fulfilled.
- Hence devotion and commitment to improvement rather than mere institutional control is the basis for devising procedures and instruments for assuring quality.
- The right balance between the health and growth of an institution needs to be struck.
- The IQAC has to ensure that whatever is done in **MSAJCE** for education is done efficiently and effectively with high standards.
- In order to do this, the IQAC will have to first establish procedures and modalities to collect data and information on various aspects of institutional functioning.
- The coordinators of the IQAC and the secretary have a major role in implementing these functions.
- The IQAC may derive major support from the already existing units and mechanisms that contribute to the listed functions.
- The operational features and functions discussed are broad based to facilitate institutions towards academic excellence and institutions may adapt them to their specific needs.

## 11. Criteria 1 - Curricular Aspects
- **MSAJCE** ensures effective curriculum delivery through a well-planned and documented process.
- **MSAJCE** adheres to the academic calendar including for the conduct of continuous internal evaluation (CIE).
- Teachers of **MSAJCE** participate in activities related to curriculum development and assessment of the affiliating University and are represented on academic bodies.
- The percentage of Programmes in which Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) or elective course system has been implemented is documented.
- The number of Add on and Certificate programs offered is tracked, along with brochures and additional information.
- The average percentage of students enrolled in these Add-on and Certificate programs as against the total number of students is evaluated.
- **MSAJCE** integrates crosscutting issues relevant to Professional ethics, Gender, Human Values, Environment and Sustainability into the Curriculum, supported by lists of courses and events organized under these areas.
- The average percentage of courses that include experiential learning through project work, field work, or internships is measured, along with lists of Memorandums of Understanding (MoU) with relevant organizations.
- The percentage of students undertaking project work, field work, or internships is also documented.
- **MSAJCE** obtains feedback on the syllabus and its transaction at **MSAJCE** from various stakeholders, and the feedback process is clearly established.

## 12. Criteria 2 - Teaching Learning and Evaluation
- The average enrolment percentage and the average percentage of seats filled against seats reserved for various categories are monitored.
- **MSAJCE** assesses the learning levels of the students and organises special Programmes for advanced learners and slow learners.
- The Student to Full time teacher ratio is maintained.
- Student centric methods, such as experiential learning, participative learning and problem solving methodologies are used for enhancing learning experiences, and teachers use ICT enabled tools for effective teaching and learning processes.
- The ratio of mentors to students for academic and other related issues is tracked.
- The average percentage of full time teachers against sanctioned posts during the last five years is recorded.
- The average percentage of full time teachers with advanced degrees such as Ph.D., D.M., M.Ch., D.N.B Super speciality, D.Sc., or D.Litt., and the average teaching experience of full time teachers in the same institution are documented.
- The mechanism of internal assessment is transparent and robust in terms of frequency and mode.
- The mechanism to deal with internal and external examination related grievances is transparent, time-bound and efficient.
- Programme and course outcomes for all Programmes offered by **MSAJCE** are stated and displayed on the website and communicated to teachers and students, and the attainment of these outcomes is evaluated by **MSAJCE**.
- The average pass percentage of students and the Student Satisfaction Survey results over the last five years are evaluated.

## 13. Criteria 3 - Research, Innovations and Extension
- Grants received from Government and non-governmental agencies for research projects, endowments, and Chairs in **MSAJCE** are recorded.
- The percentage of teachers recognized as research guides and the percentage of departments having research projects funded by government and non-government agencies are documented.
- **MSAJCE** tracks the number of sanctioned posts for faculty.
- **MSAJCE** has created an ecosystem for innovations and has initiatives for the creation and transfer of knowledge.
- The number of workshops and seminars conducted on Research Methodology, Intellectual Property Rights, and entrepreneurship is monitored.
- The number of Ph.Ds. registered per eligible teacher, the number of research papers per teacher in the Journals notified on the UGC website, and the number of books and chapters in edited volumes or books published and papers published in national or international conference proceedings per teacher are recorded.
- Extension activities are carried out in the neighbourhood community sensitizing students to social issues for their holistic development.
- The number of awards and recognitions received for extension activities from government recognized bodies is monitored.
- The number of extension and outreach Programmes conducted by **MSAJCE** through NSS, NCC, Red Cross, YRC, including Swachh Bharat, AIDS awareness, and Gender issues, or those organised in collaboration with industry, community and NGOs, is documented.
- The average percentage of students participating in these extension activities is evaluated.
- Collaborative activities for Research, Faculty Exchange, Student Exchange, or Internships, and the number of functional MoUs with institutions, universities, industries, and corporate houses are tracked.

## 14. Criteria 4 - Infrastructure and Learning Resources
- **MSAJCE** ensures adequate infrastructure and physical facilities for teaching and learning, such as classrooms, laboratories, computing equipment, and facilities for cultural activities, sports, indoor and outdoor games, gymnasium, and yoga centre.
- The percentage of classrooms and seminar halls with ICT-enabled facilities such as smart class and LMS is documented.
- The average percentage of expenditure, excluding salary for infrastructure augmentation, is calculated.
- The Library is automated using an Integrated Library Management System.
- **MSAJCE** has subscriptions for e-resources, and tracks the average annual expenditure for the purchase of books, e-books and subscriptions to journals and e-journals, along with the percentage per day usage of the library by teachers and students.
- **MSAJCE** frequently updates its IT facilities including Wi-Fi, monitors the Student to Computer ratio, and ensures sufficient bandwidth of internet connection.
- The average percentage of expenditure incurred on maintenance of physical and academic support infrastructure excluding the salary component is documented, and there are established systems and procedures for maintaining and utilizing physical, academic and support facilities like the laboratory, library, sports complex, computers, and classrooms.

## 15. Criteria 5 - Student Support and Progression
- The average percentage of students benefited by scholarships and free ships provided by the Government, as well as **MSAJCE** or non-government agencies, is tracked.
- Capacity building and skills enhancement initiatives are taken by **MSAJCE**.
- The average percentage of students benefitted by guidance for competitive examinations and career counselling offered by **MSAJCE** is monitored.
- **MSAJCE** has a transparent mechanism for timely redressal of student grievances including sexual harassment and ragging cases.
- The average percentage of placement of outgoing students, students progressing to higher education, and students qualifying in state, national, or international level examinations is documented.
- The number of awards and medals for outstanding performance in sports and cultural activities is tracked, along with students' representation and engagement in various administrative, co-curricular and extra-curricular activities, and the average number of sports and cultural events and competitions in which students participated.
- Alumni contribution to the development of **MSAJCE** through financial or other support services is also evaluated.

## 16. Criteria 6 - Governances, Leadership and Management
- The governance of **MSAJCE** is reflective of and in tune with the vision and mission of **MSAJCE**.
- Effective leadership is visible in various institutional practices such as decentralization and participative management.
- The institutional strategic and perspective plan is effectively deployed.
- The functioning of the institutional bodies is effective and efficient as visible from policies, administrative setup, appointment and service rules, and procedures.
- The implementation of e-governance in areas of operation is ensured.
- There are effective welfare measures for faculty and staff, and the average percentage of teachers provided with financial support to attend conferences, workshops, and towards membership fees of professional bodies is monitored.
- The average number of professional development and administrative training programs organized by **MSAJCE** for teaching and non-teaching staff, and the average percentage of teachers undergoing online or face-to-face Faculty Development Programmes are tracked.
- **MSAJCE** uses a Performance Appraisal System for teaching and non-teaching staff.
- Internal and external financial audits are regularly conducted, and institutional strategies for mobilisation of funds and the optimal utilisation of resources are deployed.
- The IQAC contributes significantly for institutionalizing quality assurance strategies and processes.
- **MSAJCE** reviews its teaching learning process, structures and methodologies of operations and learning outcomes at periodic intervals through the IQAC and records the incremental improvement.
- Quality assurance initiatives of **MSAJCE** are continuously monitored.

## 17. Criteria 7 - Institutional Values and Best Practices
- Measures are initiated by **MSAJCE** for the promotion of gender equity.
- **MSAJCE** has facilities for alternate sources of energy and energy conservation measures, management of degradable and non-degradable waste, and water conservation facilities.
- **MSAJCE** implements Green Campus Initiatives, and quality audits on environment and energy are regularly undertaken.
- **MSAJCE** has a disabled-friendly and barrier-free environment, and makes efforts to provide an inclusive environment.
- **MSAJCE** focuses on sensitization of students and employees to constitutional obligations.
- There is a prescribed code of conduct for students, teachers, administrators and other staff, and periodic programmes are conducted in this regard.
- **MSAJCE** celebrates and organizes national and international commemorative days, events and festivals.
- Best practices are successfully implemented by **MSAJCE**, along with institutional distinctiveness in relevant areas.
