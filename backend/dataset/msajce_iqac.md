# IQAC

## Overview

In line with the National Action Plan of the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) in Bangalore, institutions of higher education are encouraged to establish an Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) to ensure continuous performance evaluation, assessment, and accreditation, ultimately leading to quality upgradation. This initiative is part of the NAAC's efforts to promote quality sustenance measures in accredited institutions.

The IQAC is envisioned to be an integral part of an institution's system, working tirelessly towards achieving the goals of quality enhancement and sustenance. As quality enhancement is an ongoing process, the IQAC's role is crucial in developing a system that fosters conscious, consistent, and catalytic improvement in the institution's performance. By doing so, the IQAC will make a significant and meaningful contribution to the institution's growth during the post-accreditation phase.

During this period, the IQAC will play a vital role in channeling the institution's efforts and measures towards achieving academic excellence. By focusing on quality enhancement and sustenance, the IQAC will help the institution maintain its accredited status and strive for continuous improvement.

The guidelines outlined in the following pages are designed to facilitate the creation and operation of the Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) within the institution. This marks the first step towards internalizing and institutionalizing quality enhancement, a process that relies heavily on fostering a sense of belonging and participation among all constituents of the institution.

The success of the IQAC hinges on its ability to transcend traditional hierarchical structures and record-keeping exercises, instead functioning as a facilitative and participative voluntary system that is integral to the institution. By doing so, the IQAC has the potential to become a powerful vehicle for ushering in quality, achieved through the development of intervention strategies aimed at removing deficiencies and enhancing quality. This approach is reminiscent of quality circles in industries, which operate on similar principles.

The primary objectives of the IQAC are twofold. Firstly, it seeks to develop a system that enables conscious, consistent, and catalytic action to improve the academic and administrative performance of the institution. Secondly, it aims to promote measures that facilitate institutional functioning towards quality enhancement, achieved through the internalization of quality culture and the institutionalization of best practices.

In pursuit of these objectives, the IQAC will evolve mechanisms and procedures to ensure the timely, efficient, and progressive performance of academic, administrative, and financial tasks. Additionally, it will focus on ensuring the relevance and quality of academic and research programs, as well as promoting equitable access to and affordability of academic programs for various sections of society.

The primary objectives of the institution include the optimization and integration of modern methods of teaching and learning, ensuring the credibility of evaluation procedures, and maintaining the adequacy and functioning of support structures and services. Furthermore, the institution aims to facilitate research sharing and networking with other institutions in India and abroad.

The functions of the institution encompass the development and application of quality benchmarks and parameters for various academic and administrative activities. This involves disseminating information on quality parameters in higher education and organizing workshops and seminars on quality-related themes, as well as promoting quality circles. Additionally, the institution is responsible for documenting various programs and activities that lead to quality improvement. It also serves as a nodal agency for quality-related activities and prepares the Annual Quality Assurance Report (AQAR) to be submitted to the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) based on quality parameters.

The institution's Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) plays a vital role in facilitating a heightened level of clarity and focus in institutional functioning towards quality enhancement, thereby promoting the internalization of quality culture. The IQAC contributes to the enhancement and integration of various institutional activities, institutionalizing good practices, and providing a sound basis for decision-making to improve institutional functioning. Moreover, it acts as a change agent in the institution, fostering better internal communication.

The coordinator of the Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) plays a vital role in ensuring the effective functioning of all its members. Ideally, the coordinator should be a senior person with expertise in quality aspects, who may be a full-time functionary or a senior academic or administrator entrusted with the IQAC as an additional responsibility. To facilitate the coordinator's tasks, secretarial assistance may be provided by the administration. It is also desirable for the coordinator to possess sound knowledge about computers, their various functions, and usage for effective communication.

The operational features of the IQAC are centered around quality assurance, which is a by-product of ongoing efforts to define an institution's objectives, create a work plan to achieve them, and establish checks and balances to evaluate the degree to which each task is fulfilled. The basis for devising procedures and instruments for assuring quality lies in devotion and commitment to improvement, rather than mere institutional control. The IQAC's primary goal is to strike the right balance between the health and growth of an institution, ensuring that all educational activities are carried out efficiently and effectively with high standards. To achieve this, the IQAC must first establish procedures and modalities to collect data and information on various aspects of institutional functioning.

The coordinators of the Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) and the secretary play a pivotal role in implementing the functions that drive the institution towards academic excellence. 

The IQAC can draw significant support from existing units and mechanisms that contribute to its functions, thereby facilitating a smoother execution of its responsibilities. 

The operational features and functions of the IQAC are intentionally broad-based, allowing institutions the flexibility to adapt them to their specific needs and ultimately strive for academic excellence.
