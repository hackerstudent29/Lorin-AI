## Overview

Artificial intelligence, or AI, is a revolutionary field that focuses on creating machines capable of thinking and functioning like humans. This innovative process involves the simulation of human intelligence in machines, typically achieved through sophisticated algorithms that empower computers to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as visual perception, speech recognition, and decision-making.

Data Science is a distinct field of knowledge that applies advanced statistical and mathematical techniques to large datasets, with the primary goal of extracting valuable knowledge from them. The data in question can be structured, similar to a spreadsheet, or unstructured, such as text or video data. By combining techniques from statistics, mathematics, computer programming, and other relevant fields, data science provides a powerful tool for solving real-world problems using information technology.

The widespread exploration of the Internet has led to the development of AI and Data Science, which together deliver data-driven solutions using computational principles, methods, and systems. These modern computational systems have the capability to extract information from data, revealing insights and capabilities that are typical of human intelligence, including the ability to think, learn, and take action.

Through this program, students will develop a unique blend of cross-disciplinary skills that span statistics, computer science, machine learning, and logic. The primary objective of the B.Tech Artificial Intelligence and Data Science program is to empower students with a strong foundation in statistical and mathematical reasoning, as well as expertise in machine learning, knowledge discovery, and visualization skills.

The Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Data Science sectors in India are poised for explosive growth over the next five years, from 2025 to 2030. As AI-driven solutions continue to rely on data analytics to make better decisions, the demand for professionals in these fields is expected to grow significantly.

Across various industries, including healthcare, finance, e-commerce, telecommunications, automotive, and manufacturing, there is a notable increase in the adoption of Artificial Intelligence (AI). This surge in AI adoption is expected to lead to the widespread use of AI for tasks such as predictive analytics, personalized services, data-driven decision-making, and automation. As a result, AI-driven solutions will become integral to core business functions, including human resources, finance, and customer service.

The Indian government has launched several initiatives to boost AI research, startups, and adoption across public services, including Digital India, the National AI Strategy, and the National AI Mission. These initiatives have positioned India as a global leader in AI for sectors such as smart cities, healthcare, and agriculture. Furthermore, there is a rising demand for data-driven decision-making, with applications in customer segmentation, fraud detection, and supply chain optimization.

The growth of AI-powered startups is expected to accelerate in the coming years, with the number of AI startups projected to double in the near future. This growth will create numerous job opportunities for AI engineers, data scientists, and product managers. As industries such as finance, retail, e-commerce, and manufacturing increasingly adopt AI-based automation, there will be a greater need for professionals who can streamline operations, reduce costs, and offer personalized experiences to customers.

Looking ahead to the period between 2025 and 2030, several key AI and data science job roles are expected to emerge in India, driven by the growing demand for AI and data-driven solutions across various sectors.

In the field of data science, professionals are responsible for analyzing large data sets to derive actionable insights, building predictive models, and helping organizations make data-driven decisions. To excel in this role, data scientists must possess skills in Python, R, SQL, machine learning algorithms, statistical analysis, and data visualization tools such as Tableau and Power BI, as well as deep learning. The demand for data scientists in India is expected to remain high, with industries across healthcare, finance, retail, and e-commerce driving this demand.

Machine learning engineers, on the other hand, focus on building and deploying machine learning models, implementing algorithms, and optimizing model performance for real-world applications. These professionals must have expertise in Python, TensorFlow, Keras, PyTorch, neural networks, and cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud, as well as algorithm optimization. As companies adopt more AI solutions, the demand for machine learning engineers is expected to rise, particularly in startups and tech giants.

AI research scientists conduct cutting-edge research in AI, focusing on deep learning, reinforcement learning, computer vision, and natural language processing (NLP). To succeed in this role, AI research scientists must possess skills in machine learning algorithms, Python, mathematical modeling, research publications, and deep learning frameworks. With growing AI investment in India, especially from global tech companies and research labs, there will be a high demand for AI research scientists.

Data analysts collect, process, and analyze data to uncover trends, create dashboards, and support decision-making with data-driven insights. These professionals must have skills in Excel, SQL, Python, data visualization tools such as Tableau and Power BI, and statistical tools like R and SAS. As companies focus more on data-driven decision-making, the demand for data analysts in sectors like finance, retail, and healthcare is expected to increase.

Data engineers design, construct, and maintain large-scale data pipelines, working with big data technologies to ensure data is stored, processed, and accessible. To excel in this role, data engineers must possess skills in SQL, Python, Hadoop, Spark, Kafka, cloud services such as AWS and Google Cloud, and ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) processes. With the growth of big data and cloud computing, the demand for data engineers will continue to rise, especially in data-driven companies.

AI product managers oversee the development and deployment of AI-powered products, managing cross-functional teams of engineers and data scientists. These professionals must have skills in product lifecycle management, agile methodology, machine learning, project management, and business strategy. The rise in AI product development across industries will create more opportunities for AI product managers.

Business intelligence (BI) developers design and develop BI tools to collect, store, and analyze business data, helping businesses make strategic decisions using AI-driven insights. To succeed in this role, BI developers must possess skills in SQL, Power BI, Tableau, data visualization, reporting tools, and Python. As companies shift to data-driven strategies, the demand for BI developers will increase, particularly in e-commerce and retail.

Natural language processing (NLP) engineers work on algorithms and models that allow machines to process and understand human language, such as chatbots, virtual assistants, and sentiment analysis. These professionals must have skills in Python, NLP frameworks such as NLTK, SpaCy, and Hugging Face, machine learning, deep learning, and text mining. With the rise of AI chatbots, voice assistants, and text analytics, demand for NLP engineers will grow in e-commerce, finance, and telecommunications.
