# B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science

## 1. Overview
The B.Tech program in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science provides a comprehensive education in these rapidly growing fields. Artificial intelligence is the process of creating machines to think like humans. It is a term used to describe the simulation of human intelligence processes in machines. It typically involves sophisticated algorithms that enable computers to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as visual perception, speech recognition, and decision making.

Data Science is a field of knowledge which applies advanced statistical and mathematical techniques to large datasets in order to extract knowledge from them. This data can be structured like a spreadsheet but can also be unstructured like text or video data. Data science is the application of techniques from statistics, mathematics, computer programming, and other fields to solve real-world problems using information technology.

Due to the exploration of the Internet, Artificial Intelligence and Data Science deliver data-driven solutions using computational principles, methods, and systems for extracting information from data and modern computational systems that reveal capabilities of insight, way of thinking, learning, and action that are typical of human intelligence.

Since 2015, the B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science has emerged as one of the most high-growth, dynamic, and lucrative careers in technology. This course aims at providing not only the core technologies such as artificial intelligence, data mining, and data modeling but also gives intensive inputs in areas of machine learning and big data analytics. Through this course, the students will gain cross-disciplinary skills across fields such as statistics, computer science, machine learning, and logic. Graduates may have career opportunities in healthcare, business, e-commerce, social networking companies, climatology, biotechnology, genetics, and other important areas. The major focus of this programme is to equip students with statistical, mathematical reasoning, machine learning, knowledge discovery, and visualization skills.

The specialized branch deals with the core principles and ethical issues in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence and motivates students to explore the cutting-edge applications related to Big Data, Neural Networks, and Deep Learning. Applications like Alexa, Siri, Erica, Sophia, Tesla’s driverless car, and Chat-bots are becoming common. AI-powered robots can take over human life-endangering jobs like firefighting, deep-sea oil drilling, mining works with high mortality rates, and more.

## 7. Programme Outcomes (PO)
The Programme Outcomes define the foundational abilities of the students. - **Programme Outcome (PO1) - Engineering Knowledge**: Engineering knowledge requires applying mathematics, science, engineering fundamentals, and an engineering specialization to solve complex engineering problems. - **Programme Outcome (PO2) - Problem Analysis**: Problem analysis involves identifying, formulating, reviewing research literature, and analyzing complex engineering problems to reach substantiated conclusions using first principles of mathematics, natural sciences, and engineering sciences. - **Programme Outcome (PO3) - Design/Development of Solutions**: For the design and development of solutions, students must design solutions for complex engineering problems and design system components or processes that meet specified needs with appropriate consideration for public health and safety, as well as cultural, societal, and environmental considerations. - **Programme Outcome (PO4) - Conduct Investigations**: Conducting investigations of complex problems requires using research-based knowledge and methods, including the design of experiments, analysis and interpretation of data, and synthesis of information to provide valid conclusions. - **Programme Outcome (PO5) - Modern Tool Usage**: Modern tool usage involves creating, selecting, and applying appropriate techniques, resources, and modern engineering and IT tools, including prediction and modeling, to complex engineering activities with an understanding of their limitations.

- **Programme Outcome (PO6) - The Engineer and Society**: Further Programme Outcomes emphasize the engineer and society, applying reasoning informed by contextual knowledge to assess societal, health, safety, legal, and cultural issues and the consequent responsibilities relevant to professional engineering practice. - **Programme Outcome (PO7) - Environment and Sustainability**: Environment and sustainability focus on understanding the impact of professional engineering solutions in societal and environmental contexts, demonstrating the knowledge of and need for sustainable development. - **Programme Outcome (PO8) - Ethics**: Ethics involves applying ethical principles and committing to professional ethics, responsibilities, and norms of engineering practice. - **Programme Outcome (PO9) - Individual and Team Work**: Individual and teamwork require functioning effectively as an individual, and as a member or leader in diverse teams and multidisciplinary settings. - **Programme Outcome (PO10) - Communication**: Communication ensures the ability to communicate effectively on complex engineering activities with the engineering community and society at large, including comprehending and writing effective reports, designing documentation, making effective presentations, and giving and receiving clear instructions. - **Programme Outcome (PO11) - Project Management and Finance**: Project management and finance involve demonstrating knowledge and understanding of engineering and management principles and applying these to one's own work, managing projects, and working in multidisciplinary environments. - **Programme Outcome (PO12) - Life-long Learning**: Finally, life-long learning recognizes the need for and the ability to engage in independent and life-long learning in the broadest context of technological change.

## 8. Job Profiles

### AI and Data Science Growth in India by 2025 – 2030
The Artificial Intelligence and Data Science sectors in India are experiencing explosive growth expected between 2025 and 2030. As AI-driven solutions rely on data analytics to make better decisions, the demand for professionals in these fields is expected to grow significantly. This growth is driven by increased AI adoption across industries such as healthcare, finance, e-commerce, telecommunications, automotive, and manufacturing. AI will be used for tasks like predictive analytics, personalized services, data-driven decision-making, and automation, integrating into core business functions like human resources, finance, and customer service. Government support and initiatives, such as Digital India, the National AI Strategy, and the National AI Mission, further boost AI research, startups, and adoption across public services, making India a global leader in AI for smart cities, healthcare, and agriculture. The rising demand for data-driven decision-making leads to advancements in customer segmentation, fraud detection, and supply chain optimization. The growth in AI-powered startups is expected to double, creating numerous job opportunities for engineers, data scientists, and product managers as industries increasingly adopt AI-based automation to streamline operations, reduce costs, and offer personalized customer experiences.

### Key AI and Data Science Job Roles in India (2025-2030)
Key Artificial Intelligence and Data Science job roles in India include Data Scientists who analyze large data sets to derive actionable insights, build predictive models, and help organizations make data-driven decisions. This role requires skills in Python, R, SQL, machine learning algorithms, statistical analysis, data visualization tools like Tableau and Power BI, and deep learning. The demand for data scientists will remain high across healthcare, finance, retail, and e-commerce. Machine Learning Engineers build and deploy machine learning models, implement algorithms, and optimize model performance for real-world applications. They require skills in Python, TensorFlow, Keras, PyTorch, neural networks, cloud platforms like AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud, along with algorithm optimization. The demand for machine learning engineers will rise as companies adopt more AI solutions, especially in startups and tech giants.

AI Research Scientists conduct cutting-edge research in AI, focusing on deep learning, reinforcement learning, computer vision, and natural language processing. Their skill set includes machine learning algorithms, Python, mathematical modeling, research publications, and deep learning frameworks. Growing AI investments from global tech companies and research labs will result in high demand for AI research scientists. Data Analysts collect, process, and analyze data to uncover trends, create dashboards, and support decision-making with data-driven insights. They need skills in Excel, SQL, Python, data visualization tools, and statistical tools like R and SAS. As companies focus on data-driven decision-making, the demand for data analysts in sectors like finance, retail, and healthcare will increase.

Data Engineers design, construct, and maintain large-scale data pipelines, working with big data technologies to ensure data is stored, processed, and accessible. They require skills in SQL, Python, Hadoop, Spark, Kafka, cloud services, and ETL processes. The growth of big data and cloud computing will drive the demand for data engineers in data-driven companies. AI Product Managers oversee the development and deployment of AI-powered products and manage cross-functional teams of engineers and data scientists. They must be skilled in product lifecycle management, agile methodologies, machine learning, project management, and business strategy. The rise in AI product development across industries will create more opportunities for AI product managers.

Business Intelligence Developers design and develop tools to collect, store, and analyze business data to help businesses make strategic decisions using AI-driven insights. They need skills in SQL, Power BI, Tableau, data visualization, reporting tools, and Python. The shift to data-driven strategies will increase the demand for BI developers, particularly in e-commerce and retail. Natural Language Processing Engineers work on algorithms and models that allow machines to process and understand human language, such as chatbots, virtual assistants, and sentiment analysis. They require skills in Python, NLP frameworks like NLTK, SpaCy, and Hugging Face, machine learning, deep learning, and text mining. The rise of AI chatbots and text analytics will grow the demand for NLP engineers in e-commerce, finance, and telecommunications.

### Industry-Specific AI and Data Science Job Trends
Industry-specific Artificial Intelligence and Data Science job trends span multiple sectors. In healthcare, there is a focus on diagnostics, personalized treatment, drug discovery, robot-assisted surgery, bioinformatics, healthcare data analysis, and medical AI applications. The finance and fintech sectors utilize AI for fraud detection, credit scoring, automated trading, and personalized financial services within banks, insurance companies, and fintech startups. E-commerce and retail focus on personalization, dynamic pricing, inventory optimization, and recommendation systems. The automotive and autonomous vehicles sector is advancing in autonomous driving, vehicle-to-everything communication, predictive maintenance, computer vision, sensor data processing, and AI-powered automotive solutions. The telecommunication sector is prioritizing network optimization, customer service chatbots, predictive maintenance, 5G, the Internet of Things, and edge computing.

### Expected Growth and Salary Trends
The job market growth for Artificial Intelligence and Data Science in India is expected to grow at a Compound Annual Growth Rate of thirty to forty percent. By 2030, India could see hundreds of thousands of AI and data science job openings in tech companies, startups, and industries adopting data-driven transformation. Salary trends for these roles are highly competitive. Entry-level roles for zero to two years of experience typically range from 6 to 12 Lakhs per annum. Mid-level roles for three to five years of experience range from 12 to 25 Lakhs per annum. Senior-level roles for over five years of experience can range from 25 to 50 Lakhs per annum. AI Research Scientists often see salaries ranging from 30 to 70 Lakhs per annum.

### Skills and Certifications Required
To succeed in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science jobs, professionals require a strong foundation of skills and certifications. Key skills include Python, R, and SQL for data manipulation and machine learning algorithms. Professionals must be proficient in machine learning frameworks like TensorFlow, Keras, and PyTorch. Experience with cloud platforms such as AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure is essential for deploying AI models. Additionally, expertise in big data tools like Hadoop, Spark, and Kafka is critical. Candidates are encouraged to pursue advanced certifications from platforms like Coursera, edX, and Udacity. Highly regarded certifications include the Google Professional Machine Learning Engineer, AWS Certified Machine Learning Specialty, the Data Science Specialization by IBM, and the Deep Learning Specialization by Andrew Ng.
