# National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF) Overview

## 1. NIRF Overview
The National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF) is a methodology adopted by the Ministry of Education, Government of India, to rank institutions of higher education across the country. **MSAJCE** participates in NIRF rankings annually and submits data across three categories, which are Engineering, Overall, and Innovation.

## 2. NIRF 2026 Submissions
**MSAJCE** has submitted its data for the 2026 NIRF rankings. The submission document for the Engineering category can be accessed at https://www.msjace-edu.in/uploads/nift/**MSAJCE**-ENGG-2026.pdf. The submission document for the Overall category is available at https://www.msjace-edu.in/uploads/nift/**MSAJCE**-OVERALL-2026.pdf. The submission document for the Innovation category can be downloaded from https://www.msjace-edu.in/uploads/nift/**MSAJCE**-INNOV-2026.pdf.
