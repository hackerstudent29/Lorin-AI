# Learning Centre (Library)

## 1. Overview
The Library is the Heart of every academic Institution. **MSAJCE** encourages all students and staff members to make the best use of the library that has been carefully built up since the inception of **MSAJCE** as a cardinal support service of the Institute. The prime objective of the library is to provide the right information to the right readers at the right time in the right manner by bibliographic sources and full text sources in the form of print as well as digital in the disciplines of Science, Engineering, Technology, Humanities, Management, and General fields. These are kept in separate places such as the stack section, reference Section, and Periodical Section to support the scholarly and informational needs of the Institute Community. The library is housed in a spacious area of 8978 Square feet covering the Ground Floor and First Floor. It is fully computerized with more than 29853 books and 1885 reference volumes stacked for the benefit of staff and students. The library subscribes to around 35 specialized Printed journals and 20 magazines. It is also a member of DELNET and the J-Gate database is subscribed yearly.

The library regularly features the addition of new titles and volumes in all subject areas. Every effort has been made to acquire all of the titles that the faculty have recommended. Reputable newspapers, weekly, and fortnightly publications are subscribed to so that the students can stay up to date on current events. Bar-coded Technology, open source software called Koha, internet connectivity, and WIFI facility are all available to users.

## 2. Library Resources and Stack Collection Details
The library holds 29853 volumes comprising 5628 titles. There are 1885 reference books and 3790 e-books available. The library subscribes to 35 national journals, 20 magazines, and 5 daily newspapers. There are 106 back volumes, 356 CD-ROMs, and 260 project reports. It provides access to 1379 e-journals through DELNET and 1800 international journals through the Gale Database.

## 10. Institution Membership
**MSAJCE** is a member of the Developing library Network (DELNET), an International Library Network, which allows users to avail any book, Xerox or soft copy of any journal article through Inter Library Loan at https://delnet.in/. It is also a member of J-Gate Database, which provides access to more than 50684 journals online at https://jgateplus.com/.

## 11. Rules & Regulations
All the Staff and Students are members of **MSAJCE** Library. All students are eligible to borrow 18 books, and all staff are eligible to borrow books. Reference books and periodicals will not be issued, and the Library ID is not transferable. Members should notify the Librarian immediately in case of any loss of cards or change of address. A user may reserve a book in circulation and will be notified as soon as the reserved books are returned. The book will be retained at the circulation Desk for one day from the date of return. Books will be issued for a short duration of 30 days only. After 30 days, an overdue fine of 1 rupee per day per book will be charged for the first seven days. For 8 to 14 days after the due date, the fine is 2 rupees per day per book, and from 15 days onwards, the fine is 5 rupees per day per book. Silence should be observed in the Library. Users should leave their personal belongings like bags, briefcases, notebooks and files at the entrance of the Library. The loss of books should be reported immediately to the Librarian. If the borrower is unable to replace the lost book, double the cost amount will be recovered from the borrower. All the books must be returned to the Library in time for the physical stock verification of library books. Students are advised to check whether the book contains all the pages intact and then borrow. At the time of returning, if any damage or defect is found, the borrower will be held responsible. 

## 12. Membership Borrowing Eligibility
Teaching Staffs are entitled to borrow 10 books for a loan period of 30 days. Under Graduate Students and Post Graduate Students are entitled to borrow 18 books for a loan period of 30 days. Supporting and Administrative Staff are entitled to borrow 4 books for a loan period of 30 days.
