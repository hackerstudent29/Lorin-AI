# Transport Infrastructure and Bus Services

## 1. College Bus Facility
**MSAJCE** is easily accessible from Chennai Central Railway Station, Tambaram Railway Station and CMDA Bus station by the state operated transport service. In addition, **MSAJCE** provides its own transportation for students and staff residing in and around the campus. The Institute operates 9 buses for the benefit of students and the staff. The transport facility is available from various places in Chennai, Chengalpattu, Kanchipuram and Thiruvallur districts to the institute on all working days. The bus facilities are being utilized for local social service activities, Sports, NCC and NSS activities, Placement and Training activities, industrial visits and educational trips. Our transport facilities ensure that our students and staff are able to reach **MSAJCE** in time with utmost care and ease.

A transport committee, headed by a Convener, is formed to take care of the smooth operation of buses. The staff in-charge of the buses, which includes one teaching and one supporting staff, ensure the smooth functioning of the buses. The Transport committee meets once in Six months and the Principal and the staff in-charge of the buses attend the meeting. Dr. K. P. ## 4. Nearest Bus Stops & Public Transport
The park is well-served by **Metropolitan Transport Corporation (MTC)** buses with dedicated stops at the park entrance and inside the campus:
- **Key Bus Stops**: "Siruseri I.T. Park", "SIPCOT", and "Navalur".
- **Major Bus Routes**:
    - **570 Series** (570, 570S, 570V, 570C, AC-570): Connects to CMBT (Koyembedu), Guindy, and Velachery.
    - **102 Series** (102, 102X, 102-EXT, 102A, 102CT): Connects to Tambaram, Broadway, and Adyar.
    - **19 Series** (19, 19B, 19K, 19GCT, B19): Connects to Adyar, T. Nagar, and Kelambakkam.
    - **105 Series** (105, 105A): Connects to Tambaram and surrounding areas.
    - **109 Series** (109, 109T, 109X): Connects to Kovalam and Broadway along the ECR to OMR links.
    - **523 Series** (523, 523A): Connects to Thiruvanmiyur and surrounding OMR localities.
    - **Other Key Routes**: 519 (T. Nagar), 555G (Chengalpattu), 588 (Adyar), 589 (Velachery), 599 (T. Nagar), 221 (Central), 568B (Velachery), and MAA-2.
- **Suburban Train**: The nearest railway stations are **Perungudi** (approx. 8 km) and **Tambaram** (approx. 12 km), requiring a bus or auto-rickshaw connection.
