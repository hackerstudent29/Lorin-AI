# Anti-Ragging Policy at Mohamed Sathak A.J. College of Engineering (MSAJCE), Siruseri, Chennai

The Anti-Ragging Policy at Mohamed Sathak A.J. College of Engineering (MSAJCE) in Siruseri, Chennai, strictly prohibits any indiscipline activities by students that cause or are likely to cause annoyance, hardship, physical or psychological harm, or raise fear or apprehension in any fresher or other student. It is strictly forbidden to ask any student to perform acts they would not ordinarily do, especially those that generate a sense of shame, torment, or embarrassment affecting their physical or psychological well-being. Furthermore, the college explicitly bans any acts by senior students that prevent, disrupt, or disturb the regular academic activities of freshers or other students. Exploiting the services of any student to complete academic tasks assigned to an individual or group is also considered a severe violation of the policy.

The anti-ragging framework comprehensively covers and prohibits any acts of financial extortion or forceful expenditure burdens placed on freshers or other students. The policy maintains a zero-tolerance approach toward any physical abuse, encompassing all its variants such as sexual abuse, homosexual assaults, stripping, forcing obscene and lewd acts or gestures, causing bodily harm, or posing any danger to health or person. Additionally, any abuse through spoken words, emails, posts, or public insults is strictly penalized, especially acts involving deriving perverted pleasure, or a vicarious or sadistic thrill from actively or passively participating in the discomfiture of a fresher or any other student. Finally, the college strictly forbids any acts that affect the mental health and self-confidence of a student, whether or not the intent is to derive sadistic pleasure or to show off power, authority, or superiority over others.

## Punishments and Disciplinary Actions

Students found guilty of ragging at MSAJCE face severe disciplinary actions and punishments. The administration reserves the right to withhold or withdraw scholarships, fellowships, and any other associated benefits from the offenders. Guilty individuals may be debarred from appearing in any tests, examinations, or other evaluation processes, and their academic results may be formally withheld. Furthermore, offenders can be debarred from representing the institution in any regional, national, or international meets, tournaments, or youth festivals.

More severe consequences for ragging violations include immediate suspension or expulsion from the college hostel, as well as the outright cancellation of their admission to the college. Depending on the severity of the offense, the administration may rusticate the student from the institution for a period ranging from one to four semesters. In the most extreme cases, the punishment involves permanent expulsion from the institution, along with consequent debarring from admission to any other educational institution for a specified period.
