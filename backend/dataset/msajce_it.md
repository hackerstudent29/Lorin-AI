# IT

## Overview

The role of Information Technology is vital in every aspect of human life, touching each individual in ways that may not be immediately apparent to those outside the field. Information technology is essentially the study and use of systems for storing, retrieving, and processing information. The introduction of computers has revolutionized the business world, ensuring a smooth operation of businesses and enabling them to stay abreast of changes in the global markets more quickly than ever before.

As a result of the rapid development of information technology, globalization has increased, bringing the world closer together. Information can now be stored and shared quickly and easily across the globe, facilitating easier and more affordable communication. The widespread use of the internet, social media, and mobile devices has made it possible for people to connect with each other from anywhere in the world, with platforms like Skype enabling real-time communication.

The field of Information Technology encompasses a range of key areas, including Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Cloud Computing, Networking, Mobile computing, and Block Chain, among others. These areas offer tremendous job opportunities for those interested in pursuing a career in IT.

The B.Tech. - Information Technology program at Mohamed Sathak A.J. College of Engineering is a four-year regular program or a three-year lateral entry program, consisting of eight semesters for regular students and six semesters for lateral entry students. The program has a total intake of 60 students, divided into 30 government seats and 30 management seats.

The Bachelor of Engineering program at Mohamed Sathak A.J. College of Engineering follows the 10+2 system of education, which requires students to have completed their higher secondary education. To be eligible for the program, students must have secured a pass in the core subjects of Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics in their qualifying examination.

The curriculum and syllabus for the program are available in two regulations: the 2021 Regulation and the 2017 Regulation, which provide a comprehensive outline of the course structure and content. For those seeking more information on the program, additional details can be found by clicking on the provided link.

Graduates of the program can look forward to a wide range of career opportunities, including roles as planners, designers, constructors, and operators, as well as stewards of the environment, innovators and integrators of technology, managers of risk, and leaders in public policy. Additionally, they can also pursue careers in the public sector undertaking and government sector.

The program also provides a strong foundation for higher studies, offering students a solid base to pursue advanced degrees and further their academic and professional pursuits.

## Vision

At Mohamed Sathak A.J. College of Engineering, the vision is to become a centre of excellence that transforms students into proficient IT professionals through sustainable practices. This vision is supported by the mission to impart core fundamental knowledge and necessary skills in Information Technology through innovative teaching and learning methodologies. The mission also aims to inculcate critical thinking, ethics, lifelong learning, and creativity needed for industry and society.

The programme educational objectives are designed to prepare graduates for designing and developing efficient Information Technology systems and software with creativity and sustainability. Graduates will also be skilled in the use of modern tools for critical problem-solving and analyzing industrial and societal requirements. Furthermore, they will be prepared with managerial and leadership skills for career and starting up their own firms.

In terms of program-specific outcomes, graduates will be able to apply the acquired knowledge and adapt modern tools to provide innovative solutions in Networking and Cyber Security applications. They will also be able to design, implement, and test mobile apps and software solutions for various automation applications. Ultimately, the programme outcomes aim to equip graduates with engineering knowledge, enabling them to apply the knowledge of mathematics, science, engineering fundamentals, and an engineering specialization to the solution of complex engineering problems.

The vision of Mohamed Sathak A.J. College of Engineering encompasses a comprehensive set of program outcomes that aim to equip students with a wide range of skills and knowledge. One of the key objectives is to enable students to identify, formulate, and analyze complex engineering problems, reaching substantiated conclusions using the fundamental principles of mathematics, natural sciences, and engineering sciences, as outlined in PO2, Problem Analysis.

In addition to problem analysis, the college also focuses on the design and development of solutions for complex engineering problems, as stated in PO3, Design/Development Of Solutions. This involves designing system components or processes that meet specified needs, taking into account public health and safety, as well as cultural, societal, and environmental considerations. Furthermore, students are expected to conduct investigations of complex problems using research-based knowledge and methods, including the design of experiments, analysis and interpretation of data, and synthesis of information to provide valid conclusions, as outlined in PO4, Conduct Investigations of Complex Problems.

The college also emphasizes the importance of modern tool usage, as stated in PO5, Modern Tool Usage. This involves creating, selecting, and applying appropriate techniques, resources, and modern engineering and IT tools, including prediction and modeling, to complex engineering activities, with an understanding of their limitations. Moreover, students are expected to apply reasoning informed by contextual knowledge to assess societal, health, safety, legal, and cultural issues relevant to professional engineering practice, as outlined in PO6, The Engineer and Society.

Lastly, the college aims to instill in its students an understanding of the impact of professional engineering solutions on societal and environmental contexts, as stated in PO7, Environment and Sustainability. This involves demonstrating knowledge of, and the need for, sustainable development, and applying this knowledge to real-world problems.

The vision of the institution encompasses a broad range of skills and values that its students are expected to embody. One of the key principles is the application of ethical standards, as outlined in PO8, where students are encouraged to commit to professional ethics and responsibilities, adhering to the norms of engineering practice. This emphasis on ethics is complemented by the ability to function effectively in diverse teams, as stated in PO9, where students are expected to work efficiently as individuals and as members or leaders in multi-disciplinary settings.

Effective communication is another vital aspect of the vision, as highlighted in PO10, where students are expected to convey complex engineering ideas to both the engineering community and society at large. This involves developing skills such as writing effective reports and design documentation, making presentations, and giving and receiving clear instructions. Furthermore, the institution aims to equip its students with knowledge and understanding of engineering and management principles, as outlined in PO11, enabling them to manage projects and work effectively in multi-disciplinary environments.

Ultimately, the vision of the institution is to produce graduates who are prepared for life-long learning, as stated in PO12. This involves recognizing the need for continuous learning in the context of technological change and having the ability to engage in independent learning. By instilling these values and skills, the institution aims to produce well-rounded engineers who are equipped to succeed in their careers and make meaningful contributions to society.

## Job Profiles

Job opportunities for graduates of Mohamed Sathak A.J. College of Engineering are abundant in both India and abroad, spanning across public and private sectors. Various multinational corporations, including both service and product companies, offer positions such as System Engineers, Software Professionals, Network Engineers, System Analysts, System Administrators, Project Leaders, and System Architects. Additionally, opportunities are available in the education sector, allowing graduates to pursue careers as academicians.

The Information Technology industry in India is poised for significant growth over the next five years, driven by a combination of domestic and global trends. According to forecasts, the IT sector in India is expected to experience substantial employment opportunities from 2025 to 2030.

In India, businesses across various sectors are undergoing a significant digital transformation, driven by the adoption of cutting-edge technologies such as cloud computing, artificial intelligence, big data, the Internet of Things (IoT), and blockchain. This shift is expected to lead to an increased demand for IT professionals in specialized fields, including cloud engineers, data scientists, AI specialists, and DevOps engineers.

The cloud market in India is experiencing rapid growth, fueled by both private enterprises and government initiatives. As a result, cloud platforms such as Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud will create a high demand for skilled professionals, including cloud architects, cloud administrators, and cloud security specialists. Furthermore, hybrid cloud solutions will continue to see significant growth, generating opportunities in cloud migration, cloud operations, and cloud infrastructure management.

Artificial intelligence and machine learning are transforming multiple industries in India, including healthcare, finance, manufacturing, and retail. By 2030, the country is expected to see an increasing demand for data scientists, AI researchers, machine learning engineers, and AI-enabled software developers. Startups and established companies are heavily investing in AI for automation, natural language processing, and intelligent systems, making AI ethics, deep learning, and AI model deployment essential skills.

The increasing number of cyberattacks, data breaches, and regulatory requirements, such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Personal Data Protection Bill, will continue to drive the demand for cybersecurity experts in India. Roles such as ethical hackers, incident response professionals, security architects, and Security Operations Center (SOC) analysts are expected to be in high demand.

The Fintech industry, which combines finance with technology, will continue to expand in India, with a significant rise in demand for professionals in blockchain development, cryptocurrency, and smart contracts. The country's push toward a cashless economy and the adoption of digital payment systems will require skilled professionals, including blockchain developers, smart contract auditors, and Decentralized Finance (DeFi) specialists.

The rollout of 5G networks in India will create demand for skilled professionals, including 5G network engineers, telecommunications architects, and telecom software developers. The IoT will also see a boom in smart cities, agriculture, and healthcare, requiring professionals skilled in IoT development, IoT security, and embedded systems.

Government initiatives such as Digital India, Smart Cities Mission, and Make in India will boost employment opportunities in e-Governance, digital infrastructure development, and smart city projects. There will be roles for IT project managers, data engineers, IoT developers, and cloud solutions architects to support these initiatives.

India will continue to be a global hub for IT outsourcing and business process outsourcing (BPO). The demand for roles such as application developers, IT consultants, systems integrators, and support specialists will remain steady. Although automation and AI will impact certain outsourcing jobs, areas like cloud services, big data analytics, and AI/ML services will increase employment in more advanced technical roles.
