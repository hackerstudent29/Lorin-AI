# Sathak Innovation and Incubation Foundation

## 1. Overview
- The main objects to be pursued by the company upon its incorporation are to promote commerce, science, education and research by helping, establishing and incubating startups, individuals, teams with innovative ideas to make prototypes and to convert them into Products.
- Another objective is to promote a culture of innovation driven entrepreneurship in the fields of science, engineering, technology, commerce, agriculture, health care, medicine, pharmacy, education, arts, sports and other various fields.
- Furthermore, it aims to establish, help establish and or manage infrastructure, laboratories, to make and test prototypes or products, pilot plant production facilities, software development facilities, training and human resource development centre, propogate new scientific and technologies development and innovation from idea stage to full scale commercial production or any part of this chain.
- For the above purposes to assist, co-operate or collaborates with any establishment, laboratory, technology business incubators centre, organisation or institution in India or abroad.

## 2. Incubation Cell
- Various programs and initiatives to support aspiring entrepreneurs and early-stage startups are provided.
- Incubation services typically include 5000 square feet physical office space, several 100, 400, 700 seater halls for conducting many events in Entrepreneurship, infrastructure, and shared resources for startups.
- There are funding opportunities to startups through grants, seed funding, or connections to potential investors.
- Mentorship programs where experienced professionals and industry experts provide guidance and support to entrepreneurs and startups are available.
- There is access to a network of investors, venture capitalists, and angel investors who are interested in supporting innovative ideas and startups.
- The cell organizes events, workshops, and training programs to foster innovation, entrepreneurial skills, and knowledge sharing.
- It also facilitates partnerships with academic institutions, industry bodies, and government agencies to create synergies and opportunities for startups.

## 3. National Innovation and Startup Policy
- The Vision is to cater the needs of student entrepreneurs with innovative ideas there by introducing a culture of entrepreneurship inside campus which will strengthen our education system and there by promoting the national economic and social growth.
- The Mission is to develop an ecosystem with required infrastructure that can enable students and faculty to innovate and prototype their potential ideas with industrial standards and support from Government, industry and reputed academic institutions around the world and help them to realize their potentials.

## 4. Objectives
- The Short Term Objectives include helping student groups to prototype their ideas, improving innovation, creative and design thinking among student community, and providing incubation facility for faculty driven start-ups and student or Alumni start-ups.
- Other short term objectives are to organize FDP, seminars and workshops, distinguish talks for students, Faculty and Alumni and promote entrepreneurial culture, and to strengthen institute industry interaction cell activity and effectively use the outcomes for achieving the mission.
- The Long Term Objectives are to associate with DST, CII, MSME and other academic institutions for transferring world class facility for **MSAJCE** IEDC activities, and to improve quality of research work among students and to attain patent, which can be commercially used in production.
- Further long term objectives are to provide a platform for students to develop innovative products with global recognition and generate business opportunities, generate revenues through consultancy work and student start-ups, and spread awareness to students and faculty regarding IPR related activities.

## 5. Strategic Partnership Linkage
- **MSAJCE** has strategic partnership linkage with Entrepreneurship Development Institute of India (EDII) in Chennai.
- Another linkage is with the National Innovation Foundation (NIF) of India to submit ideas and apply for schemes.
- **MSAJCE** also procures fund from AICTE for the Entrepreneurship Development Cell.

- The Outcomes include all student and faculty having entrepreneurship orientation, and being motivated to start any entrepreneurial activity, aiming for 5 IPR or Innovations developed ideas per year, and 5 Student or Early-Stage Start-ups to be formed per year.

Additionally, the outcomes expect 250 beneficiaries accessing the infrastructure and facilities per day, month and Year, 25 percent innovators identified with 25 awarded or recognized and 25 supported.

- Finally, 5 percent Representatives of experts and entrepreneurial students across Departments and Disciplines are targeted, along with 5 Beneficiaries Referred to Incubators or investors for further support through Start-up Cell, and 10 Beneficiaries generated under various schemes and programs leveraged and converged at Start-up Cell.
- These are verified through Quarterly News Letter, Biannual Survey, and Monthly progress report.
- The Activities include 5 Education or Skill certification programs on Entrepreneurship, IIPR, Innovation, 25 workshops, awareness, and market outreach events, orientation, advocacy meetings, and 12 networking events organized.
- They also include 5 skill and competency development training programs, FDPs or EDPs organized, 5 research studies related to Entrepreneurship conducted, 1 national and regional award and campus Hackathon like events organized, incentivizing Entrepreneurship and Innovation with services and facilities like Start-up Manual, policies, tool kits, and 1 percent of total budget per year spent against total institution revenue for start-up, along with Budget allocation and Spend ratio for the start-up mandate in the institute.

## 11. Institution Innovation Council (IIC-**MSAJCE**)
- **MSAJCE** Innovation Council (IIC-**MSAJCE**) had been constituted in **MSAJCE** as per the guidelines of the Ministry of Human Resource Development Innovation Cell (MIC).
- The Ministry of Human Resource Development (MHRD), Government of India has established MHRD's Innovation Cell (MIC) to systematically foster the culture of Innovation amongst all Higher Education Institutions (HEIs).
- The primary mandate of MIC is to encourage, inspire and nurture young students by supporting them to work with new ideas and transform them into prototypes while they are in their formative years.
- MIC has envisioned encouraging the creation of Institution's Innovation Council (IICs) across selected HEIs.
- A network of these IICs will be established to promote innovation in **MSAJCE** through multitudinous modes leading to an innovation promotion eco-system in the campuses.
- The major focus of IIC is to create a vibrant local innovation ecosystem, and to support the Start-up Mechanism in HEIs.
- It aims to prepare the institute for Atal Ranking of Institutions on Innovation Achievements Framework, establish Function at Ecosystem for Scouting Ideas and Pre-incubation of Ideas, and develop better Cognitive Ability for Technology Students.

## 12. Functions of IICs
- The functions include conducting various innovation and entrepreneurship-related activities prescribed by the ministry of Education's Innovation cell as and when announced by it.
- It will identify and reward innovations and share success stories.
- It will also organize periodic workshops, seminars, and interactions with entrepreneurs, investors, professionals and create a mentor pool for student innovators.
- Furthermore, it will network with peers and national entrepreneurship development organizations, create an Institution's Innovation portal to highlight innovative projects carried out by **MSAJCE**'s faculty and students, and organize Hackathons, idea competitions, and mini-challenges with the involvement of industries.
