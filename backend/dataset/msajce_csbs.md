# Computer Science and Business System (CSBS)

## 1. Overview
- The B.Tech in Computer Science and Business System is a specialized undergraduate engineering program designed to blend core computer science education with insights into business systems and management.
- It is a relatively new course introduced in collaboration with industry leaders like Tata Consultancy Services to bridge the gap between traditional computer science education and the dynamic needs of the IT and business world.
- This program aims to enhance the relevance of the Computer Science program in India to meet the future demands of the IT industry, whose landscape is rapidly changing in the era of Business 4.0.
- The curriculum is designed to ensure that students get better exposure to emerging subjects such as analytics, deep learning, machine learning, cloud computing, business analytics, financial management, economics, Internet of Things, and design thinking, and also to develop an equal interest and appreciation towards humanity, value-added science, and other interrelated topics.
- The students graduating from this program will be in high demand by industries and will be highly productive.
- The primary objective is to produce graduates who are not only technically sound in computer science but also deeply understand the fundamentals of business, economics, and management.
- The program features a balanced curriculum that covers core computer science subjects like data structures, algorithms, operating systems, database management systems, and artificial intelligence along with business and management subjects such as economics, financial management, and business process management.
- Designed in partnership with Tata Consultancy Services, it places a strong emphasis on employability and industry readiness.
- Focus areas include problem-solving using computational thinking, business analytics and decision-making, and IT systems for business applications.
- Furthermore, soft skills training in communication, teamwork, and entrepreneurial thinking is heavily integrated.
- Top recruiters for graduates include major companies like TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Accenture, and CTS, as well as startups and FinTech companies like FSS.
- The benefits of this program over regular Computer Science and Engineering include more interdisciplinary exposure, better suitability for roles in tech-enabled business functions, and greater alignment with industry expectations, especially in IT services and consulting.

## 2. Vision
- The vision is to be a center of excellence for transforming students into proficient IT Professionals with business skills through sustainable practices.

## 3. Mission
- The mission involves imparting core fundamental knowledge and necessary skills in Information Science and Business through innovative teaching and learning methodologies.
- It aims to inculcate the business thinking, ethics, lifelong learning, and creativity needed for both the industry and society.
- Furthermore, the mission cultivates the students with all-around competencies for their careers, higher education, and self-employability.

## 4. Programme Educational Objectives (PEOs)
- **Programme Educational Objective (PEO1)**: The Programme Educational Objectives state that graduates will be prepared for analyzing, designing, developing, and testing software solutions and products with creativity and sustainability.
- They will also be skilled in the use of modern tools for critical problem solving and analyzing industrial and societal requirements.
- In addition, graduates will be prepared with managerial and leadership skills for their careers and for starting up their own firms.

## 5. Program Specific Outcomes (PSOs)
- The Program Specific Outcomes emphasize that engineering graduates will be able to develop creative solutions by adapting emerging technologies and tools for real-time applications in the industry.
- **Program Specific Outcome (PSO2)**: They will also apply their acquired knowledge to develop software solutions and innovative mobile apps for various automation applications.

## 6. Programme Outcomes (PO)
- The Programme Outcomes define the foundational abilities of the students.
- **Programme Outcome (PO1) - Engineering Knowledge**: Engineering knowledge requires applying mathematics, science, engineering fundamentals, and an engineering specialization to solve complex engineering problems. - **Programme Outcome (PO2) - Problem Analysis**: Problem analysis involves identifying, formulating, reviewing research literature, and analyzing complex engineering problems to reach substantiated conclusions using first principles of mathematics, natural sciences, and engineering sciences.
- **Programme Outcome (PO3) - Design/Development of Solutions**: For the design and development of solutions, students must design solutions for complex engineering problems and design system components or processes that meet specified needs with appropriate consideration for public health and safety, as well as cultural, societal, and environmental considerations.
- **Programme Outcome (PO4) - Conduct Investigations**: Conducting investigations of complex problems requires using research-based knowledge and methods, including the design of experiments, analysis and interpretation of data, and synthesis of information to provide valid conclusions.
- **Programme Outcome (PO5) - Modern Tool Usage**: Modern tool usage involves creating, selecting, and applying appropriate techniques, resources, and modern engineering and IT tools, including prediction and modeling, to complex engineering activities with an understanding of their limitations.
- **Programme Outcome (PO6) - The Engineer and Society**: Further Programme Outcomes emphasize the engineer and society, applying reasoning informed by contextual knowledge to assess societal, health, safety, legal, and cultural issues and the consequent responsibilities relevant to professional engineering practice.
- **Programme Outcome (PO7) - Environment and Sustainability**: Environment and sustainability focus on understanding the impact of professional engineering solutions in societal and environmental contexts, demonstrating the knowledge of and need for sustainable development.
- **Programme Outcome (PO8) - Ethics**: Ethics involves applying ethical principles and committing to professional ethics, responsibilities, and norms of engineering practice.
- **Programme Outcome (PO9) - Individual and Team Work**: Individual and teamwork require functioning effectively as an individual, and as a member or leader in diverse teams and multidisciplinary settings.
- **Programme Outcome (PO10) - Communication**: Communication ensures the ability to communicate effectively on complex engineering activities with the engineering community and society at large, including comprehending and writing effective reports, designing documentation, making effective presentations, and giving and receiving clear instructions.
- **Programme Outcome (PO11) - Project Management and Finance**: Project management and finance involve demonstrating knowledge and understanding of engineering and management principles and applying these to one's own work, managing projects, and working in multidisciplinary environments.
- **Programme Outcome (PO12) - Life-long Learning**: Finally, life-long learning recognizes the need for and the ability to engage in independent and life-long learning in the broadest context of technological change.

## 7. Job Profiles

### Employment Growth in Computer Science and Business Systems by 2025 - 2030 in India
- The employment opportunities for professionals in Computer Science and Business Systems in India over the next five years will see significant growth, driven by the country’s increasing digitalization, the rise in e-commerce, the adoption of artificial intelligence, and the transformation of business processes.
- With India positioning itself as a hub for both IT services and business innovation, there will be a strong demand for professionals who can blend technical expertise with a solid understanding of business systems.
- Several key drivers will fuel this employment growth.
- The digital transformation of businesses is leading companies across sectors to embrace automation, AI, cloud technologies, and big data to optimize their operations.
- With the continued growth of Enterprise Resource Planning systems like SAP, Oracle, and Microsoft Dynamics, and a shift towards Software as a Service models, professionals will be needed to implement, maintain, and integrate these solutions.
- The growth of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and analytics means businesses increasingly rely on data-driven decision making, boosting the demand for data analysts, data scientists, and business intelligence professionals who can transform raw data into actionable insights.
- Cloud computing and digital platforms are expanding as businesses migrate to the cloud, creating a demand for cloud architects, cloud engineers, and business analysts who can help integrate these solutions into operations, particularly within hybrid and multi-cloud environments.
- The growth of e-commerce and retail transformation requires professionals to build online platforms, optimize user experiences, and integrate AI-powered customer service tools.
- In enterprise digitalization, the expansion of FinTech, healthcare transformation, and smart manufacturing will demand professionals skilled in developing digital payment systems, blockchain technologies, telemedicine platforms, and integrating IT systems with industrial processes.
- Finally, increased cybersecurity threats and strict regulatory compliance requirements mean companies need experts in cyber defense, data protection, and managing risk and privacy.

### Key Roles in High Demand
- A Business Systems Analyst identifies business needs, designs systems to meet those needs, and ensures that technical solutions align with business goals by understanding both business requirements and the technical aspects of IT systems using skills in systems design, ERP systems, and agile methodologies.
- A Data Scientist or Data Analyst collects, analyzes, and interprets large datasets to help businesses make informed decisions by combining business acumen with data analysis and statistical modeling using tools like Python, R, SQL, and big data platforms. A Cloud Architect or Cloud Engineer designs, implements, and manages cloud solutions that help businesses transition to or optimize their cloud environments, requiring an understanding of both business and technical sides of cloud computing across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud.
- An ERP Consultant or Implementation Specialist implements and customizes ERP systems like SAP and Oracle, interacting with business stakeholders and technical teams to ensure the system meets organizational needs through business process modeling and project management.
- An AI and Machine Learning Engineer develops machine learning algorithms, artificial intelligence tools, and models to solve business challenges such as demand forecasting, customer service automation, and fraud detection using skills like Python, TensorFlow, and natural language processing.
- A Cybersecurity Specialist protects business systems from cyber threats, ensuring the security of sensitive data and networks by implementing firewalls, intrusion detection systems, and encryption technologies.
- A Business Intelligence Analyst uses data analytics tools to help businesses make strategic decisions by generating reports, creating dashboards, and analyzing trends using SQL, Tableau, and data mining techniques.
- A Blockchain Developer designs and develops decentralized applications using blockchain technology for use in finance, supply chains, and business contracts through platforms like Ethereum and smart contracts.
- A FinTech Professional designs, implements, and optimizes financial technologies for payments, lending, insurance, and investment systems, working on both the technical side and business strategy using digital payments and data analytics.

### Sectors with High Employment Potential
- The Information Technology services sector in India will continue to be a major driver of job opportunities in this field, including roles in cloud computing, business process outsourcing, and ERP implementation.
- As India’s e-commerce market grows, there will be an increasing demand for professionals skilled in data analytics, customer experience design, and supply chain management.
- The rapidly growing financial services and FinTech sector offers opportunities in digital payments, blockchain, insurtech, and investment technology.
- In healthcare, as systems embrace digital health, telemedicine, and AI for diagnostics, professionals with expertise in both business systems and IT will be in high demand.
- Lastly, the smart manufacturing revolution driven by the Internet of Things, AI, and cloud computing will create roles focused on integrating IT systems with industrial processes.
