# CSBS

## Overview

The B.Tech in Computer Science and Business System, or CSBS, is a specialized undergraduate engineering program that uniquely combines core computer science education with valuable insights into business systems and management. This innovative program was introduced in collaboration with industry leaders, including Tata Consultancy Services, or TCS, to address the gap between traditional computer science education and the evolving needs of the IT and business world.

The primary objective of the CSBS program is to enhance the relevance of computer science education in India, preparing students to meet the future demands of the rapidly changing IT industry in the era of Business 4.0. To achieve this, the curriculum is carefully designed to provide students with comprehensive exposure to emerging subjects such as Analytics, Deep learning, Machine Learning, Cloud Computing, Business Analytics, Financial Management, Economics, and Internet of Things, as well as Design thinking.

In addition to technical expertise, the program also aims to foster an appreciation for humanity, value-added science, and other interrelated topics, ensuring that students develop a well-rounded understanding of the field. As a result, graduates of the CSBS program are expected to be in high demand by industries and will be well-equipped to make a productive contribution to their chosen field.

The Computer Science and Business Systems program at Mohamed Sathak A.J. College of Engineering is designed to provide students with a balanced curriculum that combines core computer science subjects with business and management principles. This comprehensive approach includes the study of data structures, algorithms, operating systems, database management systems, artificial intelligence, and machine learning, as well as economics, financial management, and business process management.

The program has been developed in partnership with Tata Consultancy Services (TCS), a leading industry player, to emphasize employability and industry readiness. The curriculum focuses on key areas such as problem-solving using computational thinking, business analytics and decision-making, and IT systems for business applications. Additionally, the program places a strong emphasis on soft skills training, including communication, teamwork, and entrepreneurial thinking.

The Computer Science and Business Systems program is offered as a four-year regular course or a three-year lateral entry course, with a total of eight semesters for regular students and six semesters for lateral entry students. The program has a limited intake of 30 students, with 15 seats allocated to government quota and 15 seats to management quota. To be eligible for the program, students must have completed the 10+2 system of education and secured a pass in physics, chemistry, and mathematics in their qualifying examination.

Upon completing the program, students will have a strong foundation for pursuing higher studies. The curriculum and syllabus for the program are based on the 2021 regulation, which provides students with a comprehensive and up-to-date education in computer science and business systems.

## Vision

At Mohamed Sathak A.J. College of Engineering, the vision is to establish itself as a centre of excellence that transforms students into proficient IT professionals equipped with business skills, achieved through sustainable practices.

The mission of the institution is multifaceted. Firstly, it aims to impart core fundamental knowledge and necessary skills in Information Science and Business through innovative teaching and learning methodologies. This is encapsulated in Mission M1. Furthermore, the institution seeks to inculcate business thinking, ethics, lifelong learning, and creativity needed for industry and society, as outlined in Mission M2. Additionally, the institution strives to cultivate students with all-round competencies, preparing them for career, higher education, and self-employability, as stated in Mission M3.

The Programme Educational Objectives (PEOs) of the institution are threefold. Firstly, graduates will be prepared for analysing, designing, developing, and testing software solutions and products with creativity and sustainability, as stated in PEO1. Secondly, graduates will be skilled in the use of modern tools for critical problem-solving and analyzing industrial and societal requirements, as outlined in PEO2. Lastly, graduates will be prepared with managerial and leadership skills for career and starting up their own firms, as stated in PEO3.

The Program Specific Outcomes (PSOs) of the institution are designed to equip students with specialized skills. Firstly, students will develop creative solutions by adapting emerging technologies and tools for real-time applications of industry, as stated in PSO1. Additionally, students will apply the acquired knowledge to develop software solutions and innovative mobile apps for various automation applications.

Ultimately, the Programme Outcomes (PO) of the institution aim to produce graduates who possess a strong foundation in engineering knowledge. Specifically, graduates will be able to apply the knowledge of mathematics, science, engineering fundamentals, and an engineering specialization to the solution of complex engineering problems, as stated in PO1.

The vision of Mohamed Sathak A.J. College of Engineering encompasses a comprehensive approach to engineering education, as reflected in its program outcomes. One of the primary objectives is to equip students with the ability to analyze complex engineering problems, a skill that is essential for PO2, Problem Analysis. This involves identifying, formulating, and reviewing research literature, as well as analyzing problems using the fundamental principles of mathematics, natural sciences, and engineering sciences to reach substantiated conclusions.

In addition to problem analysis, the college aims to foster the ability to design solutions for complex engineering problems, as outlined in PO3, Design/Development Of Solutions. This requires designing system components or processes that meet specified needs, taking into account public health and safety, as well as cultural, societal, and environmental considerations. Furthermore, students are expected to conduct investigations of complex problems, as stated in PO4, using research-based knowledge and methods, including the design of experiments, data analysis, and interpretation to provide valid conclusions.

The college also emphasizes the importance of modern tool usage, as specified in PO5. This involves creating, selecting, and applying appropriate techniques, resources, and modern engineering and IT tools, including prediction and modeling, to complex engineering activities, while understanding their limitations. Moreover, students are encouraged to apply reasoning informed by contextual knowledge to assess societal, health, safety, legal, and cultural issues, as outlined in PO6, The Engineer and Society, and to recognize the consequent responsibilities relevant to professional engineering practice.

Lastly, the college aims to instill an understanding of the impact of professional engineering solutions on societal and environmental contexts, as stated in PO7, Environment and Sustainability. This involves demonstrating knowledge of, and the need for, sustainable development, and recognizing the importance of considering the environmental implications of engineering solutions.

The vision of the institution encompasses a multifaceted approach to engineering education, with a strong emphasis on ethics. This is reflected in PO8, where students are expected to apply ethical principles and adhere to professional ethics and responsibilities, aligning with the norms of engineering practice. By doing so, they develop a strong moral compass that guides their decision-making and actions throughout their careers.

In addition to ethics, the institution also places a strong emphasis on individual and team work, as outlined in PO9. Students are encouraged to function effectively as individuals and as members or leaders in diverse teams, often working in multidisciplinary settings. This enables them to develop essential collaboration and leadership skills, preparing them for the complexities of the modern engineering workplace.

Effective communication is another critical aspect of the institution's vision, as stated in PO10. Students are taught to communicate complex engineering ideas and activities with clarity and precision, both within the engineering community and to the broader society. This involves developing skills in report writing, design documentation, presentations, and clear instruction-giving and receiving.

The institution also recognizes the importance of project management and finance in the engineering profession, as outlined in PO11. Students are expected to demonstrate a thorough understanding of engineering and management principles, applying these skills to manage projects and work effectively in multidisciplinary environments. By doing so, they develop the skills necessary to lead and manage projects, both as individuals and as team members.

Finally, the institution emphasizes the need for life-long learning in the rapidly changing technological landscape, as stated in PO12. Students are encouraged to recognize the importance of ongoing learning and are equipped with the skills and preparation necessary to engage in independent and life-long learning. This enables them to stay adaptable and responsive to the evolving needs of the engineering profession, ensuring their continued growth and success throughout their careers.

## Job Profiles

India is poised to experience significant growth in employment opportunities for professionals in Computer Science and Business Systems over the next five years, from 2025 to 2030. This growth is expected to be driven by the country's increasing digitalization, the rise of e-commerce, the adoption of Artificial Intelligence, and the transformation of business processes. As India positions itself as a hub for both IT services and business innovation, there will be a strong demand for professionals who possess a unique blend of technical expertise and a solid understanding of business systems.

The digital transformation of businesses is driving growth in various sectors, with companies embracing automation, AI, cloud technologies, and big data to optimize their operations. As a result, professionals skilled in implementing, maintaining, and integrating Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) systems like SAP, Oracle, and Microsoft Dynamics will be in high demand. The shift towards Software as a Service (SaaS) models further underscores the need for experts in these areas.

The increasing reliance of businesses on data for decision-making has boosted demand for data analysts, data scientists, and business intelligence professionals who can transform raw data into actionable insights. Artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) are at the heart of digital business transformations, with applications in customer relationship management, predictive analytics, and inventory management. Consequently, professionals with expertise in these technologies will be highly sought after.

The migration of businesses to the cloud continues to drive demand for cloud architects, cloud engineers, and business analysts who can help organizations seamlessly integrate cloud solutions into their operations. The rise of multi-cloud strategies, using multiple providers like AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud, will require professionals who understand both the technical and business implications of such systems.

India's growing e-commerce market has created a need for professionals to build online platforms, optimize user experiences, and integrate AI-powered customer service tools like chatbots and recommendation systems. E-commerce companies will also continue to invest in automated inventory management, logistics, and demand forecasting systems, creating demand for data scientists and systems analysts.

The expansion of India's fintech industry will lead to a rise in demand for professionals skilled in developing digital payment systems, blockchain technologies, and AI-powered financial services. The adoption of digital records, telemedicine, and AI for diagnostics in healthcare systems will require professionals with knowledge in both IT and business systems. Similarly, the adoption of Industry 4.0, IoT, and AI in manufacturing will necessitate professionals who can bridge the gap between technical systems and business operations.

As companies increasingly move operations online, the demand for professionals in cyber security will rise, with a focus on protecting business systems. This includes roles in cyber defense, data protection, and ensuring compliance with data privacy regulations. The evolving data protection laws, such as GDPR and India's Personal Data Protection Bill, will require businesses to have experts who can manage risk, privacy, and regulatory compliance.

As a Business Systems Analyst, one is responsible for identifying business needs, designing systems to meet those needs, and ensuring that technical solutions align with business goals. This role requires a deep understanding of both business requirements and the technical aspects of IT systems, including skills such as business analysis, systems design, ERP systems like SAP and Oracle, Agile methodologies, and requirements gathering.

In contrast, a Data Scientist or Data Analyst plays a crucial role in collecting, analyzing, and interpreting large datasets to help businesses make informed decisions. This role combines business acumen with data analysis and statistical modeling, requiring skills such as proficiency in Python, R, SQL, machine learning, data visualization tools like Tableau and Power BI, and big data tools like Hadoop and Spark.

A Cloud Architect or Cloud Engineer is responsible for designing, implementing, and managing cloud solutions that help businesses transition to or optimize their cloud environments. This role often requires an understanding of both business and technical sides of cloud computing, including skills such as AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, cloud architecture, serverless technologies, and cloud security.

ERP Consultants or Implementation Specialists implement and customize ERP systems like SAP and Oracle, often interacting with both business stakeholders and technical teams to ensure the system meets organizational needs. The required skills for this role include ERP software, business process modeling, project management, and change management.

AI/ML Engineers develop machine learning algorithms, artificial intelligence tools, and models to solve business challenges such as demand forecasting, customer service automation, and fraud detection. The necessary skills for this role include proficiency in Python, TensorFlow, Keras, AI algorithms, NLP, and data preprocessing.

Cyber Security Specialists protect business systems from cyber threats, ensuring the security of sensitive data and networks. This includes implementing firewalls, intrusion detection systems, and encryption technologies, requiring skills such as network security, ethical hacking, penetration testing, data encryption, and GDPR compliance.

Business Intelligence Analysts use data analytics tools to help businesses make strategic decisions, generating reports, creating dashboards, and analyzing trends. The required skills for this role include SQL, Tableau, Power BI, data mining, business analytics, and decision support systems.

Block Chain Developers design and develop decentralized applications using block chain technology, which is creating new opportunities in finance, supply chain, and business contracts. The necessary skills for this role include block chain platforms like Ethereum and Hyperledger, smart contracts, cryptography, and distributed ledgers.

Fin Tech Professionals design, implement, and optimize financial technologies for payments, lending, insurance, and investment systems. Professionals in this role work on both the technical side, such as coding, and the business side, such as strategy, requiring skills such as Fin Tech platforms, block chain, digital payments, regulatory compliance, and data analytics.

Another sector with immense potential is E-Commerce, where India's growing market will necessitate professionals skilled in data analytics, customer experience design, and supply chain management. As the e-commerce industry expands, the demand for experts in these areas is likely to increase.

The Financial Services and FinTech sector is also experiencing rapid growth, offering a range of opportunities in digital payments, blockchain, insurtech, and investment technology. This growth is expected to create a surge in demand for professionals with expertise in these areas.

The Healthcare sector is another area where professionals with a background in both business systems and IT will be in high demand. As healthcare systems increasingly adopt digital health, telemedicine, and AI for diagnostics, the need for experts who can integrate these technologies with business systems will continue to rise.

Lastly, the Manufacturing and Industry 4.0 sector is undergoing a smart manufacturing revolution, driven by the integration of IoT, AI, and cloud computing with industrial processes. This transformation is expected to create new roles focused on integrating IT systems with industrial processes, offering exciting opportunities for professionals in this field.
