# Sports and Physical Education

## 1. Physical Education Overview
Sports activities are given utmost importance as it makes the student physically strong and increases stamina and strength. **MSAJCE** has created necessary sports and games facilities for the students to strengthen physical education. Sports and games play an integral part in molding and refining the character of an individual. Studies have shown that exercise and sport activities increase the blood flow to the brain and help in increased concentration, stimulate creativity, develop better problem solving methods, and enable quick learning methods.

## 3. Objectives
The objectives of the physical education department include providing an opportunity through talent identification and developing identified talent to excel at inter-collegiate, university, and inter-university levels. Furthermore, the department aims to bring a unique culture throughout **MSAJCE** in order to promote physical fitness, talent identification, competitiveness, and brotherhood.

## 4. Sports Quota Scholarship
**MSAJCE** encourages sports enthusiasts by rendering admission through the sports quota and also by offering scholarships to district, state, and national level participants. 

## 5. Sports Facilities
**MSAJCE** has excellent infrastructure required for the development of sports skills in students. The available facilities and sports include basketball, football, cricket along with cricket nets, yoga, kabaddi, volleyball, table tennis, carrom, chess, track and field, and kho kho.

## 6. Fitness Center
There is a well-equipped gymnasium for all students and faculties featuring the latest equipment in a well-laid-out indoor gym. To encourage weight lifters, bodybuilders, and fitness seekers, a wide variety of equipment is made available. The list of equipment includes a multi gym, leg extension, preacher curl bench, multi-adjustable bench press, cable cross over machine, sitting and standing twister, leg press, spin bike, round rubberized dumbbells, rubber weight plates, olympic curl bar, olympic weight lifting bar, chrome push up bar, and a triceps rope.

## 7. Annual Sports Activities
**MSAJCE** actively participates in Anna University, South Zone, and various invitation tournaments. **MSAJCE** also conducts Anna University zonal and inter-zonal tournaments as well as an annual sports day. Additionally, **MSAJCE** hosts the Mohamed Sathak Trophy for football, the BSM Trophy for cricket, and the Fit India Cyclothon.
