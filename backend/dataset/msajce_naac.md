# NAAC

## 1. NATIONAL ASSESSMENT AND ACCREDITATION COUNCIL (NAAC)
- India boasts one of the largest and most diverse education systems globally, with significant advancements in recent years.
- The introduction of new and emerging programs, increased autonomy, and widespread expansion have collectively improved access to higher education.
- However, these developments have also raised concerns regarding the quality and relevance of higher education.
- In response to these concerns, the National Policy on Education (NPE) was introduced in 1986, followed by the Programme of Action (PoA) in 1992, which outlined strategic plans for addressing these issues and advocated for the establishment of an independent national accreditation agency.
- Consequently, the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) was established in 1994 as an autonomous institution under the University Grants Commission (UGC), with its headquarters located in Bengaluru.
- The NAAC's vision statement reflects its core mandate, which is to integrate quality assurance into the functioning of Higher Education Institutions (HEIs), thereby ensuring a commitment to excellence in education.
- The National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) operates through two key bodies: the General Council (GC) and the Executive Committee (EC), both of which comprise a diverse group of educational administrators, policymakers, and senior academicians from various segments of the Indian higher education system.
- At the helm of the NAAC's General Council is the Chairperson of the University Grants Commission (UGC), who serves as the President of the GC.
- The Chairperson of the Executive Committee, on the other hand, is an esteemed academician nominated by the President of the GC.
- This nomination process ensures that the EC is led by a highly respected and knowledgeable individual in the field of education.
- The Director of the NAAC plays a pivotal role in the organization, serving as both the academic and administrative head.
- In addition to these responsibilities, the Director is also the member secretary of both the General Council and the Executive Committee, facilitating the smooth functioning of these bodies.
- Beyond its statutory bodies and core staff, the NAAC also seeks guidance from advisory and consultative committees that are constituted as needed.
- These committees provide valuable insights and expertise, enabling the NAAC to make informed decisions and develop effective policies.
